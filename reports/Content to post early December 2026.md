## Conclusion

The bottleneck has moved again. Last round, stories waited on photos because Commons and Open Library were blocked; this round both worked, and that alone unblocked *Ella Arcangel*, the Youth Olympics story, Cebu City, and eight books. What now limits the site is the calendar: almost every high-value December story (pageants, Gilas, MLBB, Palanca, CPI, MMFF, Batang Pinoy) depends on an event that has not happened, so the practical plan is to clear the backlog this week and then publish within a day of each result, using photos cleared in advance. The policy question worth settling is whether a place photo, such as the Dakar street scene or the Araneta Coliseum, is acceptable as a Sports hero when no free athlete photo exists. A yes would make most of the December calendar publishable on the day.

The errors found this round share one cause. "Dec 2" for Iloilo, "Dec 12 and 18" for the MMFF, "Kuala Lumpur" for Gilas, and the "PVL All-Filipino semifinals" each entered the log or a report from one secondary source (a schedule aggregator, an unretrieved article, a news aggregator) and were later contradicted by a primary or second source. The two-outlet rule the site applies to stories should also apply to watchlist dates, and any date from an aggregator should carry "unconfirmed" until an organizer or a second outlet backs it.
