# Fix four stale stories, then add six

Four items on the site are now wrong or out of date, and they should be fixed before anything new is added. The **Showbiz story on Mega Manila's analog TV switch-off** still says the signal goes dark on November 22, but the NTC moved the date to **January 15, 2027**. The **SB19/BINI Grammy story** describes song submissions, but both groups are now **nominees**, the first P-pop acts ever nominated. The **Mayon guide** gives a generic "check PHIVOLCS" status line, but the volcano has been at **Alert Level 3 since October 30**. Two News briefs, the **ASEAN Summit** and the **Sara Duterte impeachment trial**, describe events that have since moved on. A few small fee fixes follow. Siquijor's fee is now a flat ₱50, and El Nido's ₱400 fee is already correct. After those fixes, the strongest additions are a fuel-price rollback brief, a 5-million-arrivals brief, Pedro Taduran's IBF title defense (which has a freely licensed photo), the QCinema awards, a combined Youth Olympics medals story, and *Yñiga* for Books. The window was quiet for team sports, and Books has no major 2026 international prize news involving Filipino authors. The most useful forward work is a dated watch list: Miss Universe (Nov 24), Miss International (Nov 25), Palanca results (likely late November), Esports Nations Cup (Nov 25–29), November inflation (early December), and Sinulog (Jan 17, 2027). A caveat applies to everything below: the notes come mostly from search-result summaries, so exact figures should be checked against the linked articles before publishing.

## Four live items now contradict the record

**Analog TV switch-off (Showbiz, `analog-tv-switch-off-mega-manila`)** is the most urgent fix, because its headline, "goes dark this weekend," will be plainly wrong on November 22. The NTC's **Memorandum Order No. 011-11-2026** moved Phase 1 (Metro Manila, Bulacan, Cavite, Rizal, Laguna, Pampanga and Bataan) from November 22, 2026 to **January 15, 2027**, and analog stations must keep simulcasting until then ([NoypiGeeks](https://www.noypigeeks.com/tech-news/analog-tv-shutdown-mega-manila-extended/); [GizGuide](https://www.gizguide.com/2026/11/ntc-delays-analog-tv-shutdown.html); [PNA on X](https://x.com/pnagovph/status/2122867893280874530)). The delay followed GMA Network's early-November request ([Manila Times](https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/11/03/business/top-business/gma-asks-ntc-to-delay-analog-tv-shutdown/2459962); [Manila Bulletin](https://mb.com.ph/2026/11/03/gma-asks-ntc-to-hold-off-analog-switch-off)). Rewrite the title and dek around the new date and keep the existing photo. The two tech outlets plus PNA are adequate, but a mainstream outlet such as Philstar or Inquirer would make the sourcing stronger.

**SB19 and BINI Grammy story (`sb19-bini-grammy-submissions`)** needs to become a nominations story. On November 16 the Recording Academy nominated SB19's **"VISA"** and BINI's **"Unang Kilig"** for **Best Asian Pop Music Performance**, which is in its first year. The other nominees are Babymetal featuring Polyphia, IVE, KiiiKii and LiSA ([Manila Times](https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/11/16/entertainment-lifestyle/show-times/bini-sb19-make-history-as-grammy-nominees/2471024); [GMA News](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/showbiz/chikaminute/1009716/sb19-bini-nominated-for-best-asian-pop-music-performance-at-the-grammys/story/); [Philstar](https://www.philstar.com/headlines/2026/11/18/2570915/bini-sb19-earn-first-grammy-nods)). The ceremony is **February 7, 2027** in Los Angeles ([Philstar Entertainment](https://www.philstar.com/entertainment/2026/11/18/2570792/what-grammy-win-would-mean-filipino-music)). This is the best-sourced item in the whole refresh. The DOT publicly congratulated both groups, which gives the story a travel-site angle ([Interaksyon](https://interaksyon.philstar.com/trends-spotlights/2026/11/17/321979/history-officially-made-dot-celebrates-sb19-bini-grammy-nominations/)). A new slug with a fresh date reads better than editing the October story in place, and the existing CC BY 2.0 SB19 photo can stay. Filipino-heritage nominees Olivia Rodrigo (seven nominations) and Bruno Mars (six) could go in a line, since two outlets confirm them ([AsAmNews](https://asamnews.com/2026/11/16/filipino-americans-olivia-rodrigo-bruno-mars-2027-grammy-nominees/); [Inquirer](https://entertainment.inquirer.net/690538/olivia-rodrigo-seven-2027-grammy-nominations)). The notes could not confirm whether BINI's Best New Artist or SB19's group submissions were nominated. Their absence from coverage suggests they were not, but the story should say nothing about them either way.

**Mayon guide (`albay-mayon-volcano`)** says "Check PHIVOLCS; the 6 km danger zone is always off-limits." That is true but understates the current risk. PHIVOLCS raised Mayon to **Alert Level 3 after a moderately explosive eruption on October 30** ([Manila Times](https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/11/02/news/mayon-continues-to-show-heightened-volcanic-activity-phivolcs/2459224)). A Nov 3 ash plume reached about 1,600 m, flights near the summit are banned, and **Level 4 would widen the danger zone to 8 km** ([Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/asia-pacific/philippines-mayon-volcano-spews-massive-ash-cloud-amid-heightened-eruption-alert/4109425)). The volcano was still at Level 3 on November 19, with 10 volcanic quakes in 24 hours ([Philstar](https://philstar.com/pilipino-star-ngayon/probinsiya/2026/11/19/2571075/bulkang-mayon-nagtala-ng-10-pagyanig)). Change the "Volcano status" quick fact to "Alert Level 3 (as of Nov 19, 2026); 6 km zone closed," and add the same line to the safety essentials and the summit FAQ. Do not mention the reported November 13 lava dome, which comes from a single aggregator. The notes found no ATV operator notice, so describe tours inside the zone as "closed under the PHIVOLCS restriction" rather than naming operators. The site's News brief on Mayon is still accurate.

**ASEAN and impeachment briefs (News).** The ASEAN brief, "Manila hosts the 49th ASEAN Summit," should now report that the summit **closed November 18** and that the Philippines **handed the 2027 chairmanship to Singapore** ([Manila Times](https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/11/17/news/philippines-hands-over-asean-2027-chairmanship-to-singapore/2471815)). ## Fuel and arrivals lead a short list of new News briefs

The window produced seven well-sourced candidates, but only four matter to travelers. The strongest is **two weeks of steep fuel rollbacks**. The DOE ordered a **₱16.80/L gasoline cut effective November 10**, tied to easing US–Iran tensions ([DOE](https://doe.gov.ph/doe-announces-major-fuel-price-rollback-of-up-to-p16-80-per-liter); [Philstar](https://www.philstar.com/business/2026/11/09/2568958/major-fuel-price-rollbacks-set-nov-10-gasoline-seen-fall-p168)). ## Conclusion

Most of what went stale comes from one kind of story: scheduled events written in the future tense ("goes dark this weekend," "make a play for," "opens trial"). Dates slip and pending outcomes resolve, so these stories age faster than briefs about things that already happened. One fix is to give any story built on a pending date or decision a follow-up date in `CONTENT_LOG.md` when it is published. Under that rule, the analog TV and Grammy problems would have come up on their own.

Photos are now a bigger constraint than sourcing. Several of the best-sourced new items (Youth Olympics, QCinema, the bowling bronze) have no freely licensed image, while weaker or older stories (Tokong at Siargao, Brillantes) do. If the bento grid had a typographic or venue-photo fallback like the Books covers, strong stories could run without waiting on Commons. Until then, recheck the PSC and PCO public-domain uploads a few days after each event.
