## Conclusion

This short window shows that the site's real bottleneck has moved from finding stories to clearing photos. Of the six strongest new candidates, four are fully sourced and waiting only on an image, and the one that is ready (Brillantes) is ready because a photo happened to exist. Two cheap fixes would help more than more research: allow commons.wikimedia.org and openlibrary.org in the session's network settings, so licences and cover IDs can be checked in the same pass as the facts, and decide whether the Showbiz and Sports grids may use a typographic fallback, as Books already does.

The corrections point the same way. The Siquijor "on arrival" error and the "Dec 2" flight dates both came from a single trade source or aggregator that a second outlet later contradicted. A simple rule would catch these: a fee, route or date enters a guide or the content log only after two outlets agree on it, and anything from a schedule aggregator stays marked "unconfirmed" until an airline or press source backs it.
