## What major Philippine news broke between Nov 9 and Nov 19, 2026? (new candidate briefs)

### Takeaway
Seven candidate briefs are well supported by two or more outlets: big DOE fuel price rollbacks (Nov 10 and Nov 17), Trump's US-Philippines "trade deal" claim with no signed deal (Nov 18), the Nov 13 reshuffle of BIR, Customs, BCDA and PEZA heads, a Senate push for a national El Niño calamity declaration (Nov 9), DOT passing 5 million visitor arrivals (reported Nov 10 to 11), the Mega Manila analog TV switch-off moved to Jan 15, 2027 (about Nov 18), and the Supreme Court striking down the seafarers' bond rule (Nov 16).
