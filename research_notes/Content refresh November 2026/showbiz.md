### Takeaway
The biggest story in the window is the Grammy nominations (Nov 16). This is a material update to the site's existing `sb19-bini-grammy-submissions` story. Miss Universe 2026 has **not** happened yet (final Nov 24). MMFF and AACA news all falls outside the window. **Grammy nominations (Nov 16, 2026) — MATERIAL UPDATE to existing story `sb19-bini-grammy-submissions` (dated 2026-10-14)**
- The Recording Academy announced the nominations for the 69th Grammy Awards on Monday, Nov 16, 2026 (Manila time). SB19 is nominated for "VISA" and BINI for "Unang Kilig" in Best Asian Pop Music Performance. These are the first Grammy nominations for any P-pop act — [Manila Times](https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/11/16/entertainment-lifestyle/show-times/bini-sb19-make-history-as-grammy-nominees/2471024); [GMA News](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/showbiz/chikaminute/1009716/sb19-bini-nominated-for-best-asian-pop-music-performance-at-the-grammys/story/); [Philstar](https://www.philstar.com/headlines/2026/11/18/2570915/bini-sb19-earn-first-grammy-nods); [Rappler](https://www.rappler.com/people/artists/sb19-bini-nominated-grammy-awards-2027/) (returned 403 when fetched; headline seen in search); [Inquirer Lifestyle](https://lifestyle.inquirer.net/586144/sb19-bini-grammy-nominations/)
- The other nominees in the category are Babymetal featuring Polyphia ("Sunset Kiss"), IVE ("Bang Bang"), KiiiKii ("404 (New Era)"), and LiSA ("Yes"), for six in total. This is the category's first year — [Philstar Entertainment](https://www.philstar.com/entertainment/2026/11/18/2570792/what-grammy-win-would-mean-filipino-music); [GMA News](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/showbiz/chikaminute/1009716/sb19-bini-nominated-for-best-asian-pop-music-performance-at-the-grammys/story/)
- The ceremony is Feb 7, 2027, at Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles — [Philstar Entertainment](https://www.philstar.com/entertainment/2026/11/18/2570792/what-grammy-win-would-mean-filipino-music)
- The Department of Tourism publicly congratulated both groups ("History has officially been made!"). This is a possible travel-site angle — [Interaksyon/Philstar](https://interaksyon.philstar.com/trends-spotlights/2026/11/17/321979/history-officially-made-dot-celebrates-sb19-bini-grammy-nominations/)
- Filipino-heritage nominees: Olivia Rodrigo has seven nominations, including Album of the Year for "you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love". Bruno Mars has six, including Album of the Year for *The Romantic*. underscores has one — [AsAmNews](https://asamnews.com/2026/11/16/filipino-americans-olivia-rodrigo-bruno-mars-2027-grammy-nominees/); [Inquirer](https://entertainment.inquirer.net/690538/olivia-rodrigo-seven-2027-grammy-nominations); [Inquirer USA](https://usa.inquirer.net/216082/filipino-artists-shine-across-2027-grammy-nominations)

**QCinema International Film Festival awards (Nov 18, 2026) — NEW candidate**
- The 14th QCinema awards night was held Nov 18, 2026, at Novotel Manila Araneta City in Cubao, Quezon City. The Filipino animated film *Ella Arcangel: Awit ng Pangil at Kuko* (dir. Mervin Malonzo) won the Asian Next Wave Grand Jury Prize and the Artistic Achievement for Animation award. Toni Cañete's *Maanaa Kanimo* won Best Filipino Film (QCShorts) and the Gender Sensitivity Award — [Daily Tribune](https://tribune.net.ph/2026/11/18/a-girl-unknown-wins-qcinema-top-prize-bleat-bags-best-short); [Screen Daily](https://www.screendaily.com/news/a-girl-unknown-ella-arcangel-win-top-prizes-at-philippines-qcinema/5222546.article) (headline confirms *Ella Arcangel*'s top prize; the body didn't load)
- The top Asian Next Wave Best Picture went to *A Girl Unknown* (dir. Zou Jing), a China–France co-production, not a Filipino film — [Daily Tribune](https://tribune.net.ph/2026/11/18/a-girl-unknown-wins-qcinema-top-prize-bleat-bags-best-short)

**Andrea Brillantes wins Best Acting Performance in London for *Laya* (reported Nov 14, 2026) — NEW candidate, borderline date**
- Brillantes won Best Acting Performance at the 2026 Women and the World International Film Festival in London. She played the lead in *Laya*, written and directed by Sigrid Andrea Bernardo, who was also nominated for Best Director. *Laya* was the Philippines' only entry in the Feature Fiction Competition — [Manila Times, Nov 14](https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/11/14/entertainment-lifestyle/show-times/andrea-brillantes-wins-acting-award-at-uk-film-festival-for-laya/2469955)
- *Laya* follows a woman who goes to a seaside town in Oslob, Cebu (a travel tie-in). It opens in select Philippine cinemas on Nov 25 — [Manila Times](https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/11/14/entertainment-lifestyle/show-times/andrea-brillantes-wins-acting-award-at-uk-film-festival-for-laya/2469955)
