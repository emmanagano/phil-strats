# Destination guide refresh: changes Sept 1 – Nov 19, 2026, Dec–Feb events, and new guide ideas

Research date: November 19, 2026. About 18 searches and fetches. Many results were travel blogs or aggregators. They are marked as such, and official confirmation is listed under Gaps where it was missing.

## Which covered destinations had fee, closure, route, ferry, typhoon or advisory changes in this period?

### Takeaway
The one confirmed, high-impact change is **Mayon Volcano (albay-mayon-volcano)**. PHIVOLCS raised it to Alert Level 3 on Oct 30, 2026 after a moderately explosive eruption, and it is still at Level 3 as of Nov 19. Entry to the 6 km Permanent Danger Zone is banned, and Level 4 would widen the zone to 8 km. I found no confirmed 2026 changes in this window for Mt Pulag, Kawasan, Oslob, the UK FCDO Mindanao advice, or new domestic air routes. Fee figures that may already be stale in the guides are El Nido, Siquijor and Oslob.

### Cited Findings

**albay-mayon-volcano (HIGH PRIORITY)**
- PHIVOLCS raised Mayon to Alert Level 3 after a moderate eruption on October 30, 2026. As of Nov 2 it remained at Level 3, with minor strombolian activity and a 300 m plume. PHIVOLCS said RSAM seismic indicators could prompt a raise to Level 4 — [Manila Times, Nov 2, 2026](https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/11/02/news/mayon-continues-to-show-heightened-volcanic-activity-phivolcs/2459224)
- An ash emission began at 08:22 AM on Nov 3, 2026, and PHIVOLCS said "Alert Level 3 prevails" — [CGTN, Nov 3, 2026](https://news.cgtn.com/news/2026-11-03/news-1RLjIg4E5qw/p.html)
- The Nov 3 plume reached about 1,600 m. Entry to the 6 km Permanent Danger Zone and flights over the summit are prohibited. Alert Level 4 would expand the danger zone to 8 km — [Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/asia-pacific/philippines-mayon-volcano-spews-massive-ash-cloud-amid-heightened-eruption-alert/4109425); [Nature World News, Nov 4, 2026](https://www.natureworldnews.com/articles/73728/20261104/mayon-volcano-stays-alert-level-3-lava-flows-new-ash-plumes-follow-its-explosive-eruption.htm)
- On Nov 19, 2026, Mayon recorded 10 volcanic earthquakes in 24 hours and stayed at Alert Level 3 — [Philstar (Pilipino Star Ngayon), Nov 19, 2026](https://philstar.com/pilipino-star-ngayon/probinsiya/2026/11/19/2571075/bulkang-mayon-nagtala-ng-10-pagyanig)
- One unverified report says a new lava dome was seen on Nov 13. It comes from a single travel-oriented site, so don't use it without PHIVOLCS confirmation — [volcanodb.com](https://volcanodb.com/mayon-volcano)

**el-nido-palawan, coron-palawan, puerto-princesa-palawan (fees)**
- El Nido's Eco-Tourism Development Fee is now ₱400 for non-Palawan visitors, up from ₱200. Sources disagree on the effective date. One cites an ETDF board bulletin on a new ordinance dated Dec 24, 2025, and another says June 2024 — [elnidoparadise.com](https://www.elnidoparadise.com/el-nido-eco-tourism-development-fee/); [palawanisland.com, "checked Oct 2026"](https://palawanisland.com/palawan-fees/). The date is uncertain, but the ₱400 rate is consistent across sources.
- Puerto Princesa Underground River: ≈₱500 entrance plus ≈₱150 city environmental fee. Coron environmental fee: ≈₱200–₱300. Both figures are from operator and blog sources, not an LGU notice — [palawanisland.com](https://palawanisland.com/palawan-fees/)
- The site already notes that there are no direct Manila–El Nido flights. I found nothing new on this.

**siquijor-island (fee)**
- Since Feb 1, 2026, Siquijor charges a tourism ecological fee of ₱50 per person for both domestic and foreign visitors. Check that the guide includes it — [Travel And Tour World](https://www.travelandtourworld.com/news/article/siquijor-philippines-introduces-tourism-ecological-fee-to-support-environmental-sustainability-and-preserve-islands-cultural-heritage/)

**cebu-kawasan-falls-oslob (fees unsettled)**
- Oslob whale shark fees conflict across 2026 sources. One gives ₱500 for Filipinos and ₱1,000 for foreigners for snorkeling (May 2026). Another gives a flat ₱1,000 plus ₱200–300 for snorkeling. Operators cite an increase from ₱500 to ₱1,000 effective March 21, 2025. Conduct rules: stay 4 m from the body and 5 m from the tail, and no touching, riding or chasing — [suroy.ph](https://suroy.ph/whale-shark-watching-oslob-cebu/); [whycebu.com](https://whycebu.com/oslob-whale-shark-price-and-entrance-fee/); [islavisit.com](https://islavisit.com/oslob-whale-shark-watching/). All are blog or operator sources, so mark the fee with ≈.
- I found no new 2026 Kawasan Falls rule or fee. One tour listing shows an optional Kawasan zipline at ₱600 — [Bohol Cebu Tours](https://www.boholcebutours.com/tour/whale-shark-interaction-kawasan-falls-with-canyoneering-day-tour/)

**baguio-city-benguet (proposed fee)**
- The Baguio city council is weighing an environmental user's fee for tourists to fund waste and sewage work. The report says passage is targeted for 2027 after consultations, so nothing is in force yet — [Daily Tribune, May 26, 2026](https://tribune.net.ph/2026/05/26/baguio-eyes-environmental-fee-for-tourists)

**mount-pulag-benguet (no change found)**
- I found no DENR closure or suspension notice for Oct–Nov 2026. Tour operators list Ambangeg climbs on Nov 28–29 and Nov 30–Dec 1, 2026 with DENR slots, which suggests the mountain is open, but that is not official — [Tanaw Explorer](https://www.tanawexplorer.com/event/mt-pulag-ambangeg-november-28-29-2026/); [Outdoor is Life](https://outdoorislife.com/event/mt-pulag-via-ambangeg-5/)
- Blog guides describe a 500-trekker daily cap, groups of 10, a required medical certificate and a guide, and a "2026 weekend camping ban". None of this was checked against DENR — [pana.ph](https://pana.ph/blog/mount-pulag-permit-climb-guide-2026); [lakbaypinas.com](https://lakbaypinas.com/mt-pulag-travel-guide-ambangeg-trail/)
- Context: two campsites closed in Feb 2025 over littering — [Daily Tribune, Feb 20, 2025](https://tribune.net.ph/2025/02/20/mt-pulag-closes-two-campsites-amid-littering-concerns)

**siargao-surfing-capital (airport)**

I could not read the article body, so whether it has opened is unconfirmed — [Context.ph, Oct 19, 2026](https://context.ph/2026/10/19/dotr-expands-siargao-airport-upgrades-ports-to-boost-tourism/); background: [Manila Bulletin, Aug 9, 2025](https://mb.com.ph/2025/08/09/siargao-airport-expansion-sets-course-for-tourism-transformation-dot)

### Inferences
- The Mayon guide needs an updated safety banner: Alert Level 3, the 6 km PDZ is off-limits, and trekking and ATV trails inside the PDZ are closed. Prefix it with "as of Nov 19, 2026" and link to PHIVOLCS bulletins, and update `guide.verified`. The ban on tours inside the PDZ follows from the PHIVOLCS restriction, but no ATV-operator notice was found.
- El Nido (₱400), Siquijor (₱50) and Oslob (≈) fees should be checked against what the guides currently say.
- Mindanao guides don't need advisory wording changes. Keep the existing FCDO notes and update the "checked" date.

## Which events in Dec 2026–Feb 2027 matter to these guides (confirmed dates only)?

### Takeaway
Only **Sinulog** has a firm 2027 date, the Grand Parade on Sunday, Jan 17, 2027, reported consistently by several outlets. Dinagyang has venues confirmed but no official dates. Ati-Atihan, Panagbenga and the San Fernando Giant Lantern Festival have only pattern-based or unofficial dates.

### Cited Findings
- **Sinulog (Cebu, links to cebu-kawasan-falls-oslob, bantayan, malapascua):** The Grand Parade is Sunday, Jan 17, 2027, starting and ending at Cebu City Sports Center. Fiesta Señor runs Jan 7–17, 2027 at the Basilica del Santo Niño. The hour-by-hour program has not been released — [suroy.ph](https://suroy.ph/sinulog-festival-in-cebu-city-a-feast-for-the-senses); [Travelbinger](https://travelbinger.com/sinulog-2027-book-early-for-cebu-citys-january-17-parade); [Philstar, Sept 16, 2026](https://www.philstar.com/cebu-news/2026/09/16/2556609/city-government-prepping-sinulog). Contestant registration deadline is Nov 30–Dec 1 — [Cebu Daily News tag page](https://cebudailynews.inquirer.net/tag/sinulog-2027). Some calendars list Jan 18, 2027, a Monday, which is wrong.
- **Dinagyang (Iloilo, iloilo-guimaras):** 10 tribes and five judging areas, up from three in 2026. Venues are the Iloilo Freedom Grandstand, the Provincial Capitol, and Quezon-Ledesma, Mabini-De Leon and Iznart streets. The Tribune article gives no dates — [Daily Tribune, Oct 3, 2026](https://tribune.net.ph/2026/10/03/dinagyang-2027-draws-10-tribes-adds-judging-areas). The competition returns to the City Proper — [IMT News](https://www.imtnews.ph/dinagyang-2027-to-return-to-iloilo-city-proper-iffi); [Daily Guardian](https://www.dailyguardian.com.ph/blog/we-listen-to-the-public-dinagyang-2027-brings-back-calle-real-vibes). Unofficial listings give Jan 22–24, 2027 (fourth weekend of January), which is unconfirmed.
- **Ati-Atihan (Kalibo, links to boracay-aklan):** The main day is traditionally the third Sunday of January, which would be Jan 17, 2027. No official 2027 confirmation was found — [Twinkl listing](https://www.twinkl.com/event/ati-atihan-2027)
- **Panagbenga (baguio-city-benguet):** It runs through February. No 2027 dates were found.
- **Giant Lantern Festival (San Fernando, Pampanga):** No official 2026 date. In 2025 the competition was held Dec 13 at Robinsons Starmills, with displays from Dec 14 to Jan 1 — [letsgopampanga.com](https://www.letsgopampanga.com/giant-lantern-festival-2025-showcases-10-barangays-7994/); [Philstar, Dec 20, 2025](https://www.philstar.com/headlines/2025/12/20/2495681/giant-lanterns-light-christmas-catholic-philippines). Wikipedia's Dec 19, 2026 start looks like a template default, so don't use it.
- **Simbang Gabi:** I didn't search for it separately. The traditional dates are Dec 16–24, but that is my background knowledge with no 2026 citation, so treat it as a gap.

### Inferences
- Guides for Cebu, Iloilo and Boracay/Aklan should flag January crowds and higher prices, with "book early" notes. Only Sinulog's date is safe to publish as fact.

### Gaps
- Official Dinagyang 2027 dates (Iloilo Festivals Foundation), Ati-Atihan 2027 (Kalibo LGU), the Panagbenga 2027 opening and parade dates (Baguio), and the San Fernando 2026 lantern competition date were not found.

## Which new destinations are trending or newly accessible that the site lacks?

### Takeaway
The evidence for "trending" places is thin. The best-sourced new item is DOT-10's launch of **Laguindingan and the Misamis Oriental dive coast** (Nov 11, 2026). Other options are tied to infrastructure or events: Antique (airport expansion), Cebu City itself (Sinulog plus new international flights), and San Fernando, Pampanga (Christmas lanterns). These are 4 suggestions. I couldn't source 5–6 well.

### Cited Findings
1. **Misamis Oriental dive coast (Laguindingan, Medina, Balingoan, Gingoog, Talisayan):** DOT Region 10 introduced Laguindingan as the province's newest dive destination at the Misamis Oriental Dive Festival in Cagayan de Oro (Nov 11–15, 2026). Laguindingan has three barangay MPAs totalling 35.75 ha (Mauswagon, Moog, Tubajon), and the gateway is Laguindingan International Airport, about 4.5 km away. Reef data (255 fish species, 53.48% live coral at Tubajon) comes from 2010–2014 surveys. Established operators include Duka Bay Resort and Mantangale Alibuag Dive Resort — [Travel And Tour World, Nov 2026, citing PIA](https://www.travelandtourworld.com/news/article/7iad842a35e8/). This is a single secondary source, so confirm with PIA or DOT-10. It pairs with the existing cagayan-de-oro-bukidnon guide. Northern Mindanao falls under FCDO "all but essential travel" — [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/philippines)
2. **Antique (San Jose de Buenavista, Panay):** A new passenger terminal holds 360 passengers, and jet operations were expected to start in Q4 2026 (as of Jan 2026). Whether jets are flying yet is unconfirmed — [Manila Standard](https://manilastandard.net/business/314693373/antique-airport-expansion-to-boost-local-tourism-in-2026.html). It sits next to the existing iloilo-guimaras and boracay-aklan guides.
3. **Cebu City heritage and Sinulog guide:** The site covers Kawasan, Oslob, Bantayan and Malapascua, but not the city. New Nagoya and Shanghai flights start Dec 1, 2026, and Sinulog falls on Jan 17, 2027 — [TRAICY](https://en.traicy.com/posts/2026092940612/); [suroy.ph](https://suroy.ph/sinulog-festival-in-cebu-city-a-feast-for-the-senses)
4. **San Fernando, Pampanga (Christmas capital):** This would be a December-only seasonal guide built around the Giant Lantern Festival, with displays from mid-December to Jan 1 based on the 2025 pattern — [letsgopampanga.com](https://www.letsgopampanga.com/giant-lantern-festival-2025-showcases-10-barangays-7994/); [TPB](https://www.tpb.gov.ph/?p=4780). The 2026 date is unconfirmed.

### Inferences
- With the Mayon and Albay guide restricted, Misamis Oriental and Cebu City are timely, lower-risk additions for the December–February season.

### Gaps
- I found no official DOT 2026–27 campaign list of trending or priority destinations. Bukidnon Airport is still under construction — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bukidnon_Airport). There is no confirmation that Antique has jet service or that the Siargao terminal has opened.
