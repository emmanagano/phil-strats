# Filipino athletes: new sports results, Nov 6–19, 2026

Scope: verified results only, Nov 6–19, 2026, excluding stories already on the site. Research was done by search on Nov 19, 2026. Commons photo metadata was pulled directly from the Commons API.

## Which new stories (Nov 6–19) are strong candidates?

### Takeaway
Four solid new stories are well sourced: Pedro Taduran retained his IBF minimumweight title (Nov 7, US time), Naomi Cesar won silver at the Dakar Youth Olympics (Nov 10), Pi Durden Wangkay won bronze there (Nov 11), and Grace Gella and Krizziah Tabora-Macatula won a bronze at the Asian Tenpin Championships (Nov 16). The two Youth Olympic medals could run as one story. Only Taduran has a strong freely licensed photo; for Cesar, Wangkay and the bowlers I found none.

### Cited Findings

**1. Pedro Taduran retains IBF minimumweight title (Nov 7 in Albuquerque, Nov 8 in Manila)**
- Taduran beat mandatory challenger Brandon Moreno by unanimous decision over 12 rounds at Kiva Auditorium, Albuquerque, New Mexico, giving Moreno his first career loss — [GMA News](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/sports/boxing/1008709/pedro-taduran-retains-ibf-title-hands-brandon-moreno-first-career-loss/story/); [Philstar "Taduran still king" (Nov 9)](https://www.philstar.com/sports/2026/11/09/2568829/taduran-still-king)
- Scorecards were 120-108, 115-113 and 115-113 (judges Anthony Romero, Esther Lopez, Mark Sanchez). Search summaries call it his fourth defense of the belt he won from Ginjiro Shigeoka. Check the defense count against the GMA article before publishing — [GMA News](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/sports/boxing/1008709/pedro-taduran-retains-ibf-title-hands-brandon-moreno-first-career-loss/story/); [Wikipedia: Pedro Taduran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Taduran)

**2. Naomi Cesar wins Youth Olympic silver in the 800 m (Nov 10, Dakar)**
- Cesar won silver in the women's 800 m at the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games in 2:09.34, behind Ethiopia's Netsanet Selemon. It was the Philippines' first medal of the Games — [Daily Tribune "Hail Cesar" (Nov 10)](https://tribune.net.ph/2026/11/10/hail-cesar); [Wikipedia timeline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_in_Philippine_sports)
- Background: she is Filipino-American. She set an 800 m national record in June 2026 — [Philstar (Jun 23)](https://www.philstar.com/other-sections/newsmakers/2026/06/23/2537074/hail-cesar-fil-am-runner-breaks-800m-national-record)
- Photo: none found on Commons.

**3. Pi Durden Wangkay wins Youth Olympic bronze in the 200 m (Nov 11, Dakar)**
- Wangkay finished third in the men's 200 m in 21.355 s. Zimbabwe's Ropafadzo Mzondo won in 21.04 and Guyana's Dequan Farrell took silver in 21.359, a photo finish with Wangkay — [GMA News (Nov 11)](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/sports/othersports/1009078/pi-durden-wangkay-wins-bronze-in-youth-olympic-games-200m-event/story/); [Daily Tribune "The power of Pi" (Nov 11)](https://tribune.net.ph/2026/11/11/the-power-of-pi)
- The nine-athlete Philippine team finished 54th on the medal table with one silver and one bronze — [Wikipedia timeline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_in_Philippine_sports)
- Photo: none found on Commons.

**4. Gella and Tabora-Macatula win doubles bronze at the Asian Tenpin Bowling Championships (Nov 16, Doha)**
- Grace Gella and Krizziah Tabora-Macatula took bronze in women's doubles at the 28th Asian Tenpin Bowling Championships in Doha with 2,717 pinfalls. A 547 final block lifted them onto the podium and stopped a Malaysian 1-2-3. Malaysia's Nur Syazwani and Adelia won gold with 2,753 — [The Star (Malaysia), Nov 16](https://www.thestar.com.my/sport/bowling/2026/11/16/malaysia-secure-1-2-finish-in-asian-championships-women039s-doubles); [Wikipedia timeline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_in_Philippine_sports)
- The championships began in Doha around Nov 11–13 — [QNA](https://qna.org.qa/en/News-Area/News/2026-11/13/28th-asian-bowling-championships-begin-in-doha); results bulletins are at [abf-online.org](https://www.abf-online.org)
- Photo: none found. The only Commons bowling hit is an unrelated Paeng Nepomuceno Hall of Fame photo.

**Minor (lower priority):** a Philippine boxing team won one silver and two bronzes at the China International Boxing Open in Zunyi, Nov 5–11. Its only source is the [Wikipedia timeline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_in_Philippine_sports); boxer names were not checked.

### Inferences
