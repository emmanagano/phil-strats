## 1. Major prizes in 2026: Filipino or Filipino-heritage winners and shortlistees

### Takeaway
No Filipino or Filipino-heritage author appears among the 25 US National Book Award finalists for 2026, and I found no 2026 Booker, Pulitzer, Newbery or Carnegie recognition for one. ## 2. Widely covered 2026 releases by Filipino and Filipino-heritage authors

### Takeaway
The most notable 2026 book with a Philippine setting is Glenn Diaz's *Yñiga*. It won the 2024 Philippine National Book Award for Best Novel in English and gets its UK/international edition from Tilted Axis on November 17, 2026, with a Coffee House Press edition listed in the US. Of the authors named in the brief, only Erin Entrada Kelly has a confirmed 2026 book. Gina Apostol's next novel is dated March 2027, and Elaine Castillo's latest, *Moderation*, came out in August 2025.

### Cited Findings
- **Glenn Diaz, *Yñiga*:** Tilted Axis lists the UK publication as November 17, 2026. In the novel, Yñiga Calinauan leaves the city after a retired army general wanted for killings of peasants and activists is caught near her home and her neighborhood burns. She goes back to M—, a small fishing town where she grew up, and has to face her family's history of disappearance. The press says it won the 2024 Philippine National Book Award (Best Novel in English) and was shortlisted for the 2020 Novel Prize (Fitzcarraldo, New Directions, Giramondo). — [Tilted Axis Press](https://www.tiltedaxispress.com/yiga/). There is also a US Coffee House Press product page (could not fetch; rate-limited): [Coffee House Press](https://coffeehousepress.org/products/yniga)
- **Erin Entrada Kelly, *The Second Life of Snap*** (HarperCollins imprint, ISBN 9780063485983): a stand-alone middle-grade survival story about Zuzu, who lives at "Bright Valley Subsidized Camp #5", and Snap, a robot guardian whose battery is running down. It is pitched to fans of *The Wild Robot*. Kelly is a two-time Newbery Medalist. — [NetGalley](https://www.netgalley.com/catalog/book/797129). It appears on a Bookshop.org list of 2026 releases by Filipino authors (per search snippet; the page returned 403). — [Bookshop.org list](https://bookshop.org/lists/2026-releases-from-filipino-authors)
- **Gina Apostol:** her sixth novel, *The Justice League of People of Color Schadenfreude*, is announced for Soho Press, March 2027 (so not 2026). — [ginaapostol.com](https://ginaapostol.com/about)
- **Elaine Castillo:** latest novel *Moderation*, release date August 5, 2025. — [Kirkus](https://www.kirkusreviews.com/book-reviews/elaine-castillo/moderation)
- **Nita Noveno, *Mud on the Moon*** (Red Hen Press, August 2026): a memoir about Filipino immigrants coming of age in Alaska. It made Poets & Writers' "New Nonfiction 2026" debut list. — [Poets & Writers](https://www.pw.org/content/the_new_nonfiction_2026)
- **Other 2026 titles on Filipino American History Month (October 2026) lists** (from search snippets; details not independently confirmed): Mae Coyiuto, *Goodbye and Everything After* (YA, Feiwel and Friends); Melissa de la Cruz, *Kingdom of Waves*; Kara Bodegón-Hikino, *Squalo and Mage vs. the Rage of the Bakunawa* (HarperAlley graphic novel, drawing on the Philippine bakunawa myth); poetry by Anna Ojascastro Guzon (*A Moth Collection*) and Genevieve DeGuzman (*Karaoke at the End of the World*). — [Publishers Weekly](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/childrens/childrens-authors/article/101528-children-s-authors-share-recommendations-for-filipino-american-history-month-2026.html); [CLMP](https://www.clmp.org/news/a-reading-list-for-filipino-american-history-month-2026/); [Bookshop.org list](https://bookshop.org/lists/2026-releases-from-filipino-authors)
- Cecilia Manguerra Brainard's *Magdalena* came out in a Portuguese translation (Editora Rua do Sabão, Brazil) in 2026. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cecilia_Manguerra_Brainard)

### Inferences
- *Yñiga* is the best "add" candidate: it has a major Philippine prize, a fresh international edition in November 2026, and a Philippine setting. The town is unnamed ("M—"), so do not link it to a specific guide via `setIn`.
- Erin Entrada Kelly is already on the site (*Hello Universe*). *The Second Life of Snap* is not set in the Philippines, so it isn't a priority for a travel-led shelf. Mention it on her existing entry at most, and only after confirming the publication date.

## 4. Literary events worth noting

### Takeaway
2026 had two big Manila book events: the NBDB's Philippine Book Festival (March 12–15, SM Megamall) and the Manila International Book Fair (September 9–13, SMX Convention Center, Pasay).

### Cited Findings
- **Philippine Book Festival 2026 (4th edition):** March 12–15, 2026, Megatrade Hall, SM Megamall, Mandaluyong. The theme was "Gubat ng Karunungan". Hours were 10am–8pm, and entry was free with registration (walk-ins accepted). The organizer was the National Book Development Board. Vogue PH gives March 12–16 in one place, so the end date conflicts slightly, but most sources say the 15th. — [Lifestyle Asia](https://lifestyleasia-onemega.com/scene/dive-into-local-literature-at-the-philippine-book-festival-2026/); [Manila Bulletin](https://mb.com.ph/2026/03/03/philippine-book-festival-returns-for-fourth-year-with-gubat-ng-karunungan); [Vogue PH](https://vogue.ph/lifestyle/culture/what-to-expect-philippine-book-festival-2026/)
- The 43rd National Book Awards ceremony was held during the festival on March 14, 2026. — [Philstar Life](https://philstarlife.com/geeky/139555-list-winning-titles-43rd-national-book-awards)
- **Manila International Book Fair 2026:** September 9–13, 2026, SMX Convention Center, Pasay City. The theme was "Get Lit: Reading in a New Light". Tickets were sold via SM Tickets or at the venue, and Wattpad marked its 20th anniversary there. — [Philstar events](https://www.philstar.com/events/690/manila-international-book-fair-2026); [L'Officiel PH](https://www.lofficielph.com/culture/your-guide-to-the-manila-international-book-fair-2026); [Interaksyon](https://interaksyon.philstar.com/hobbies-interests/2026/09/09/318922/wattpad-returns-to-manila-international-book-fair-for-20th-anniversary/)

### Inferences
- For readers planning trips, the pattern is a March festival (NBDB, SM Megamall) and a September fair (MIBF, SMX). 2027 dates are not announced, so any copy should say "usually March" and "usually September" rather than give dates.

### Gaps
- No 2027 dates found for either event.
