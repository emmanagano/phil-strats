# Destinations: new guides and guide updates for December 2026 to early 2027 (research as of 2026-11-23)

Scope: suggest 3–5 new guides tied to December, holiday or early-2027 travel, plus updates for existing guides. The 37 existing slugs were checked (`src/content/`). The late November report already recommended Cebu City (build now) and Donsol (hold), and fixed the Siquijor fee and the Iloilo/Davao route dates; this file re-checks those and adds new items without repeating settled points.

**Research conditions this session:** the Wikimedia Commons API was reachable (licences and authors below come from the API's `extmetadata`, which is the file page's own data, unlike last session). travel.state.gov returned 403, so the US advisory comes from search results. Most other facts come from search-result summaries of the cited pages. **Coordinates are from general geographic knowledge, not a fetched source, and must be checked on a map.**

## Candidate new guides

### Takeaway
Two guides are ready to build: **Cebu City** (Sinulog, Jan 17, 2027; now with several verified CC BY 4.0 / CC BY-SA 4.0 hero photos) and **San Fernando, Pampanga** (Giant Lantern Festival, the clearest December hook, with many CC BY-SA 3.0 lantern photos). **Taal heritage town, Batangas** is a reasonable third (easy Manila holiday day trip, good photos) but has only blog-level fees. **Donsol** should still wait (no season notice, and the only Commons whale shark photo is 694 px wide).

**2. San Fernando, Pampanga (Luzon; heritage/culture; new December pick)**
- The Giant Lantern Festival (Ligligan Parul) is the city's December competition of ≈20-ft lanterns on rotor light systems — [Wikipedia: Giant Lantern Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giant_Lantern_Festival); [What's On MNL](https://whatsonmnl.com/blog/giant-lantern-festival-2026-san-fernando).
- **2026 date not officially announced.** Wikipedia's infobox gives Dec 19, 2026 to Jan 1, 2027 (unsourced, single source); What's On MNL (checked Nov 4) found no city date — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giant_Lantern_Festival); [What's On MNL](https://whatsonmnl.com/blog/giant-lantern-festival-2026-san-fernando).
- 2025 pattern: competition night Saturday, Dec 13, 2025 at Robinsons Starmills; Barangay Bulaon won the 117th edition with 10 barangays competing; lanterns then shown around the city Dec 14 to Jan 1 — [PIA](https://pia.gov.ph/news/bulaon-clinches-first-championship-title-at-117th-giant-lantern-festival/); [GMA Regional](https://www.gmanetwork.com/regionaltv/community/111947/barangay-bulaon-wins-117th-giant-lantern-festival-in-pampanga/story/); [SunStar Pampanga](https://www.sunstar.com.ph/pampanga/local-news/san-fernando-giant-lanterns-all-set-for-public-exhibitions); [Philstar, Dec 21, 2025](https://www.philstar.com/headlines/2025/12/21/2495877/giant-lanterns-light-christmas-pampanga); [Robinsons Malls](https://robinsonsmalls.com/giant-lantern-festival-robinsons-starmills).
- San Fernando was named Asia's Leading Cultural City Destination 2026 at the World Travel Awards (Oct 2026) — [Daily Tribune, Oct 6, 2026](https://tribune.net.ph/2026/10/06/san-fernando-named-asias-leading-cultural-city-destination-2026) (single source; avoid as a superlative per BRAND.md, or attribute it).
- Year-round angle: Paskuhan Village has a Giant Lantern Tourist Information Center (marker photo on Commons, below); the city brands itself the "Christmas Capital" — [SunStar via PressReader](https://www.pressreader.com/philippines/sunstar-pampanga/20220926/281543704794501).

**3. Taal heritage town, Batangas (Luzon; heritage; optional third)**
- Taal Basilica (Minor Basilica of St. Martin of Tours) is free to enter; the bell tower climb charges a small fee (≈ ₱100 on a 2012 figure, likely outdated); some ancestral houses and museums charge ≈ ₱50 — [Will Fly for Food](https://www.willflyforfood.net/the-first-timers-travel-guide-to-taal-heritage-town-batangas-philippines/); [The Poor Traveler](https://www.thepoortraveler.net/2012/04/taal-heritage-town-ancestral-houses-batangas/amp); [Wikivoyage: Taal](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Taal). **All blog-level; no 2026 figures.**
- Getting there: Batangas City or Lemery buses (JAM Liner, DLTB) from PITX, Buendia or Araneta City Bus Port, get off at Taal public market; ≈ 2–3 hours — same sources as above.
- Holiday hook is weak on sources: no 2026 Christmas event found for Taal. Its value is as a short weekend trip from Manila over the holidays, plus a Heritage-category Luzon guide beyond Vigan and Intramuros.
- Hero photo candidates (Commons API): [File:Taal Basilica, Batangas, May 2026 (2).jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Taal_Basilica,_Batangas,_May_2026_(2).jpg) — 4080×3072, Ralff Nestor Nacor, CC BY-SA 4.0; [File:Allan Jay Quesada- Taal Basilica de San Martin de Tours, Taal, Batangas.JPG](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Allan_Jay_Quesada-_Taal_Basilica_de_San_Martin_de_Tours,_Taal,_Batangas.JPG) — 4839×3226 (3:2), Allan Jay Quesada, CC BY-SA 3.0; [File:Taal, Batangas (50).jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Taal,_Batangas_(50).jpg) — 3696×2448, Ryomaandres, CC BY-SA 4.0.
