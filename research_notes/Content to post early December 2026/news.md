# News candidates, Nov 19–23, 2026, plus the December watchlist

Research notes for Phil Strats News briefs. Window: November 19 to 23, 2026. Most findings come from search-result summaries; only the GMA trade story and the Manila Times El Niño story were opened in full (the Philstar "Marcos defends economic team" page timed out). **Overall: the window is thin.** Only about four items are new and verifiable: the Marcos/Trump trade clarification, the APEC trip, the El Niño state-of-calamity push, and impeachment Day 44. ## Weather

### Takeaway
I found no tropical depression or named storm inside the PAR during Nov 19–23. The big weather story is El Niño: on Nov 22 a lawmaker pushed for a state of calamity in the hardest-hit areas.

### Cited Findings

**Candidate brief 3: "Lawmaker urges El Niño state of calamity" (Nov 22, weather).**
- Rep. Leila de Lima (Mamamayang Liberal) filed House Resolution No. 1629. It urges Marcos to declare a state of calamity in areas badly hit by El Niño and a state of imminent disaster in areas expected to be hit, on NDRRMC recommendation under RA 10121. — [Manila Times, Nov 22](https://manilatimes.net/2026/11/22/news/declare-state-of-calamity-in-el-nio-hit-areas-marcos-urged/2475655)
- Citing Department of Agriculture data, the resolution puts farm damage at about ₱9.99 billion, with 324,327 farmers and 323,419 hectares affected and 288,424 metric tons of production lost. These are early figures that are still changing. — [Manila Times](https://manilatimes.net/2026/11/22/news/declare-state-of-calamity-in-el-nio-hit-areas-marcos-urged/2475655)
- Background: as of Oct 25, drought had hit nine provinces and **109 areas had declared a local state of calamity**, and a UN briefing called it a "very strong" El Niño. — [Daily Tribune, Oct 25](https://tribune.net.ph/2026/10/25/un-govt-agencies-bare-actions-to-very-strong-el-niño). PAGASA declared El Niño conditions on June 9, 2026 ([PAGASA press statement PDF](https://pubfiles.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/pagasaweb/files/piias/press_release/6a27d9c5a503a_PressStatement_EN.pdf))
- **Single source** for the resolution itself (Manila Times). The Senate already has Resolution 765 (in the existing brief). No national state of calamity has been declared.
- Travel angle: water rationing or supply notices in some provinces are possible, but no source in this window says so. Keep the "For your trip" note general (check local water advisories).

**Tropical cyclones:**
- PAGASA forecast one or two tropical cyclones for November 2026 and below-normal rainfall for most of the country. — [GMA News](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/weather/content/1007988/pagasa-sees-1-2-tropical-cyclones-in-par-in-november-2026/story/). It also forecast up to four cyclones through the end of 2026 ([GMA News](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/weather/content/1006846/pagasa-up-to-4-tropical-cyclones-may-enter-par-until-end-of-2026/story/))
- The last tropical depression found was east of Northern Luzon, outside the PAR, on Nov 7. — [Daily Tribune](https://tribune.net.ph/2026/11/07/heavy-rains-threaten-quezon-parts-of-mimaropa)

### Inferences
- If a national state of calamity is declared, it would close the El Niño watchlist item. The resolution alone is a "push", not a decision, so write it that way.

### Gaps
- No PAGASA bulletin for Nov 19–23 opened. Any storm forming after Nov 22 would be missed.
- No count of LGUs under a state of calamity as of late November.
