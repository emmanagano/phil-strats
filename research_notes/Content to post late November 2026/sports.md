## Youth Olympic Games (Dakar 2026): Filipino medals

### Takeaway
Two medals, both in athletics: Naomi Cesar won silver in the girls' 800 m and Pi Durden Wangkay won bronze in the boys' 200 m. Reports call this the Philippines' best Youth Olympic Games finish. This is the strongest candidate (well sourced, final, and athletics only has 2 stories so far). Best written as one story: "Cesar and Wangkay give the Philippines its best Youth Olympics."

### Cited Findings
- Cesar, 17, ran 2:09.34 for 800 m silver behind Ethiopia's Netsanet Selemon (Games record 2:04.43), with Morocco's Khadija Elmostaqim third in 2:10.05. She was fourth going into the final stretch and moved up to second over the last 100 m. It was the Philippines' first medal of the Games — [Daily Tribune, "Hail Cesar" (2026-11-10)](https://tribune.net.ph/2026/11/10/hail-cesar); [Manila Times via Newswav, "Cesar bags PH's first medal at Dakar Youth Olympics"](https://newswav.com/article/cesar-bags-ph-s-first-medal-at-dakar-youth-olympics-A2611_4vTydD); [World Athletics report (2026-11-10)](https://worldathletics.org/competitions/youth-olympic-games/news/youth-olympic-games-dakar-abebe-amare)
- The race was on Monday, November 9 local time; Philippine reports are dated November 10 — same sources as above.
- Wangkay won bronze in the boys' 200 m final. Zimbabwe's Ropafadzo Mzondo won gold in 21.04. Wangkay had placed fifth in his semifinal in 21.96 — [GMA News](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/sports/othersports/1009078/pi-durden-wangkay-wins-bronze-in-youth-olympic-games-200m-event/story/); [Daily Tribune, "The power of Pi" (2026-11-11)](https://tribune.net.ph/2026/11/11/the-power-of-pi); [Newswav, "Wangkay claims bronze at Dakar Youth Olympic Games"](https://newswav.com/article/wangkay-claims-bronze-at-dakar-youth-olympic-games-A2611_GSxS9g)
- The two medals beat the country's previous Youth Olympic Games best, Christian Tio's kiteboarding (twin-tip) medal at Buenos Aires 2018, and Tribune calls it the best finish in four editions — [Daily Tribune (2026-11-11)](https://tribune.net.ph/2026/11/11/the-power-of-pi). Note: Tribune's Nov 10 piece describes Tio's medal as the earlier medal but the snippets did not consistently say silver or bronze, so leave the medal color out.
- Background: both won medals at the 2025 Asian Youth Games in Bahrain (Wangkay 200 m gold, Cesar 800 m silver), and Cesar won 800 m gold at the 2025 SEA Games — [Manila Bulletin Tempo](https://tempo.mb.com.ph/?p=403694); [Philstar (2025-12-16)](https://www.philstar.com/sports/2025/12/16/2494707/cesar-lorana-star-800-m-sea-games-golds)
- The Philippines sent nine athletes, including cyclist Krog — [Daily Tribune (2026-07-13)](https://tribune.net.ph/2026/07/13/phl-parades-9-yog-bets); [Manila Bulletin (2026-05-28)](https://mb.com.ph/2026/05/28/cyclist-krog-tracksters-wangkay-and-cesar-bound-for-youth-olympic-games-in-senegal)
- Follow-up feature with the parents — [Daily Tribune (2026-11-12)](https://tribune.net.ph/2026/11/12/yog-medalists-cesar-wangkay-make-parents-proud)
