## QCinema 2026: full winners, and a free photo of *Ella Arcangel*'s director or cast?

### Takeaway
At the 14th QCinema International Film Festival awards night on **November 18, 2026**, Mervin Malonzo's animated *Ella Arcangel: Awit ng Pangil at Kuko* won the **Asian Next Wave Grand Jury Prize** and an **Artistic Achievement Award for Animation**. The China–France *A Girl Unknown* took **Best Picture**, and *BLEAT!* won **Best Short**, so the story must not call *Ella Arcangel* the overall winner. Five outlets confirm the result. A full winners list could not be retrieved, and **no freely licensed photo** of Malonzo, Bianca Umali or the film was found on Commons.
