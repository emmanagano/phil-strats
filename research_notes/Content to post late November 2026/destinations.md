## 1. Cebu City (recommended first; Visayas, heritage-culture)

### Takeaway
Cebu City is the clearest gap: three guides sit in Cebu province (Bantayan, Malapascua, Kawasan/Oslob), but none cover the city's Spanish-era heritage core, and Sinulog (Jan 17, 2027) plus new Cebu Pacific flights from Japan and China (Dec 1) make it timely for Dec–Feb.

### Cited Findings
**Festival**
- Sinulog Grand Parade: Sunday, Jan 17, 2027 (the traditional third Sunday of January), with the main venue at the Cebu City Sports Center — [Wikipedia: Sinulog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinulog); [Remitly guide](https://www.remitly.com/blog/lifestyle-culture/sinulog-festival-a-guide-to-cebus-grand-celebration/); [Travelbinger](https://travelbinger.com/sinulog-2027-book-early-for-cebu-citys-january-17-parade). - The 2027 official schedule is not out yet; the city government and committee are still preparing it — [Philstar, Sep 16, 2026](https://www.philstar.com/cebu-news/2026/09/16/2556609/city-government-prepping-sinulog); [SunStar: "village feel" in Sinulog 2027](https://www.sunstar.com.ph/cebu/officials-eye-village-feel-in-sinulog-2027); [Suroy.ph](https://suroy.ph/sinulog-festival-in-cebu-city-a-feast-for-the-senses).

## 2. Donsol, Sorsogon (Luzon / Bicol, nature-adventure)

### Takeaway
Donsol's community-run whale shark (butanding) interaction is a strong nature pick for Luzon outside the Cordillera and suits Dec–May travel, but the season has been unpredictable and fees are only reported by blogs, so treat all figures as ≈.

### Cited Findings
- Season usually runs roughly Nov/Dec to May/June, with peak sightings often Feb–May; a March 2026 review said sightings ended by late Feb 2026, so timing varies yearly — [Philippine Dives](https://philippinedives.com/dive-guide/donsol/); [TripAdvisor reviews 2026](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g1016344-d1011868-Reviews-Donsol_Whale_Shark_Interaction-Donsol_Sorsogon_Province_Bicol_Region_Luzon.html); [Pana.ph guide](https://pana.ph/guides/donsol).
- Every visitor must attend an orientation before the interaction tour — [Inquirer Plus](https://plus.inquirer.net/?p=383191).
- Fees: registration/interaction ≈ ₱300 foreigners, ≈ ₱100 Filipinos; boat ≈ ₱3,500 for up to 6–7 people (half-day, Butanding Interaction Officer included) to ≈ ₱5,000 for 3 hours; BIO fee ≈ ₱500 per boat in one guide; snorkel gear ≈ ₱300 — [Pana.ph (Tagalog)](https://pana.ph/tl/blog/tl-donsol-whale-shark-guide-wf3); [Pana.ph](https://pana.ph/guides/donsol); [Isarog bus guide](https://isarogbusbooking.ph/travel-guides/donsol-whale-shark-guide); [Just Wandering](https://justwandering.org/wp-json/wp/v2/posts/327).
- Donsol's municipal waters were declared a whale shark sanctuary, followed by the national hunting ban in 1998; WWF calls it the longest-running community-based whale shark ecotourism site in Southeast Asia — [WWF Philippines PDF](https://archive.wwf.org.ph/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/Panda-Talks_-Donsol_Community-Conservation.pdf); [Sorsogon provincial site](https://sorsogon.gov.ph/cool_timeline/whale-shark/).

### Inferences
- Coordinates (Donsol town / Whale Shark Interaction Center): ≈ [12.9086, 123.5975]. Not sourced; verify.
- Getting there (not sourced in this session): fly Manila to Legazpi/Daraga (Bicol International Airport), then van/jeepney about 1–1.5 hours via Pilar; or overnight bus from Manila. Fares need sourcing.
- The page should frame sightings as "not guaranteed" and stress the no-touch, no-flash rules; it pairs naturally with the Mayon guide (same airport).
- Not in Mindanao, so no FCDO province note.

### Gaps
- No official Donsol or Sorsogon tourism notice of 2026–27 fees or season opening.
- No sourced getting-there fares or daily budgets.
- No freely licensed Donsol whale shark photo found on Commons via search; a generic Commons whale shark photo (or a Donsol beach/town photo) may be needed.

## 3. Other candidates considered

### Takeaway
Caramoan (Camarines Sur, beaches) is a real gap but is a poor Dec–Feb pick; Kalibo's Ati-Atihan overlaps Boracay's coverage and falls on the same weekend as Sinulog. I could not research a Palawan or second Visayas candidate within this session.

### Cited Findings
- Caramoan: environmental fee ≈ ₱30 per person (paid at Guijalo/Caramoan Feeder Port); routes are a direct overnight bus from Manila (recommended in 2026) or fly to Naga then bus (about 8–9 h), or the older Sabang–Guijalo boat (≈ ₱120, up to 2 h); island-hopping boats ≈ ₱1,500–3,000 (older guide) — [Lakbay Pinas 2026 guide](https://lakbaypinas.com/caramoan-island-hopping-diy-travel-tips-itinerary/); [Philippine Beaches](https://philippinebeaches.org/?p=8334); [Pinay Solo Backpacker](https://www.thepinaysolobackpacker.com/?p=6163).
- Ati-Atihan main day is the third Sunday of January (Jan 17, 2027); 2027 date ranges differ by source (Jan 11–18 vs Jan 15–17) — [TPB listing](https://www.tpb.gov.ph/?p=4674); [Twinkl](https://www.twinkl.com/event/ati-atihan-2027); [Travelbinger](https://travelbinger.com/soot-drums-and-nonstop-street-dancing-return-to-kalibo-in-january-2027).

### Inferences
- Caramoan faces the Pacific side of Bicol; the northeast monsoon (amihan) makes Dec–Feb seas less reliable, so it would suit a March–May push instead. (Klook's only cited warning is June–October; my Dec–Feb concern is an inference, not sourced.)
- Ati-Atihan is better as an "also this weekend" note in the existing Boracay guide (Kalibo airport) than as a new guide.
- Suggested follow-ups to fill gaps: a Palawan pick (San Vicente/Long Beach or Balabac) and a Visayas pick (Gigantes Islands, Kalanggaman, or Samar's Calbiga/Sohoton). Mindanao already has 7 guides.

## 5. Siquijor tourism ecological fee

### Takeaway
Confirmed by two news outlets citing the provincial government: ₱50 on departure since Feb 1, 2026, under Ordinance No. 2022-006. The ≈ can be dropped.

### Cited Findings
- The Siquijor provincial government began collecting a tourism ecological fee on Feb 1, 2026 from visitors leaving the island: ₱50 for domestic and foreign tourists, ₱40 for seniors and students, children 6 and under and Siquijor residents (with ID or barangay certificate) exempt; Ordinance No. 2022-006; funds go to conservation, tourism infrastructure and sustainability projects — [SunStar Cebu](https://www.sunstar.com.ph/cebu/leaving-siquijor-prepare-to-pay-the-p50-tourism-ecological-fee); [PhilstarLife](https://philstarlife.com/news-and-views/672830-siquijor-tourism-ecological-fee); [DigiCast Negros](https://digicastnegros.com/siquijor-to-collect-tourism-ecological-fee-starting-feb-1).
- Some residents and businesses criticized the fee; the tourism office called it "very minimal compared to other island destinations" — [SunStar Cebu](https://www.sunstar.com.ph/cebu/leaving-siquijor-prepare-to-pay-the-p50-tourism-ecological-fee).

### Inferences
- Guide copy: "₱50 tourism ecological fee when you leave (₱40 seniors and students; under-7s free)". Paid on departure at the port.

### Gaps
- I did not see the LGU's own Facebook notice or the ordinance text; the confirmation rests on two press reports citing the provincial tourism office.
