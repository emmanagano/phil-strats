# Philippines-specific practical needs for destination guides

Research date: 17 November 2026. Most sources are travel blogs or commercial sites because official pages (US State Dept, Smartraveller) blocked or timed out on fetch. Every fee and entry rule below should carry an "as of" date in published guides.

## 1. What travelers commonly ask/search about Philippine destinations

### Takeaway
I could not get Reddit or Tripadvisor threads or autocomplete data directly. The questions that 2026 guides and FAQ pages keep answering are: how to get there (especially after the 2025–26 flight-route changes), which fees apply, whether to bring cash, when to go, and which tour, boat or route to pick. Guides for each destination should lead with logistics and fees.

### Cited Findings
- 2026 El Nido guides are organised around "the four tours" (A–D, with prices and stops) and "the new way in", meaning flights that moved from Manila to Clark. They also cover fees, cash, internet and power, and best months. — [Suroy.ph El Nido guide](https://suroy.ph/el-nido-travel-guide/)
- Palawan cost guides answer "what does El Nido/Coron/Puerto Princesa actually cost" with 4D/3N per-person budgets, transport legs, and how much cash to bring. — [Suroy.ph Palawan trip cost](https://suroy.ph/palawan-trip-cost/)
- Boracay content centres on "what fees do I pay to enter Boracay" (terminal fee, environmental fee, iPass) and whether a hotel booking is needed. — [pana.ph community Q](https://pana.ph/community/what-fees-do-i-pay-to-enter-boracay); [Tripwis, 15 things to know (2026)](https://tripwis.com/things-to-know-before-visiting-boracay-philippines)
- Siargao content centres on cash and ATMs, airport-to-General-Luna transfers, habal-habal fares, and surf vs. typhoon timing. — [PhilippineBeaches Siargao tips](https://philippinebeaches.org/?p=10409); [Suroy how to get to Siargao](https://suroy.ph/how-to-get-to-siargao/)
- eTravel is one of the most-searched entry topics, partly because of scam sites. One guide titles it "The Free Form Scammers Charge $100 For". — [bucket-list-ideas eTravel 2026](https://bucket-list-ideas.pages.dev/blog/philippines-etravel-2026-arrival-card-americans/)
- Bohol searches focus on the Chocolate Hills entrance fee and whether to tour privately, join a shared tour, or go DIY. — [Jonny Melon Chocolate Hills 2026](https://www.jonnymelon.com/chocolate-hills-bohol/); [bohol-philippines.com private/shared/DIY](https://bohol-philippines.com/chocolate-hills-tour-private-shared-or-diy/)
- Banaue searches focus on the overnight bus from Manila (Ohayami/Coleman), the environmental fee, and how to reach Batad (jeepney to the Saddle, then a trek). — [Wikivoyage Banaue](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Banaue); [12Go Manila–Banaue](https://12go.asia/en/bus/manila/banaue/)
- Coverage of Batanes is dominated by weather-related flight cancellations. — [Inquirer, Batanes flights canceled](https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/999173/news-canceled-flights-batanes-basco-miaa-cebgo)

### Inferences
- A standard "Know before you go" block for each destination would match search intent. It should cover: getting there (with the route-change date), fees (amount, where to pay, date verified), cash/ATMs, signal and power, best months, getting around, and the top tours with prices.
- The facts that age fastest are flight routes, fees, and tour prices. Show a "last checked" date for each.

### Gaps
- No direct Reddit or Tripadvisor thread data or Google autocomplete data was retrieved. Search-volume ranking of questions is unverified.

## 2. Entry requirements and destination fees

### Takeaway
Most Western nationalities get 30 days visa-free. They need a passport valid 6 months beyond the stay and an onward ticket, plus free eTravel registration (etravel.gov.ph) shortly before arrival. Destination fees are set locally and change often: El Nido ₱400, Boracay terminal ₱150 plus environmental fee ₱150/₱300, Coron about ₱200, Banaue ₱20–50, Chocolate Hills ₱100–150.

### Cited Findings (country-wide)
- eTravel is free. Register via etravel.gov.ph or the eGovPH app. The home page does not state the timing window. — [etravel.gov.ph](https://etravel.gov.ph/)
- Secondary sources disagree on timing: "within 72 hours before arrival" vs "at least three days prior". One unverified claim says mandatory registration within 72 hours applies from 1 May 2026. Flag this as uncertain. — [Wego eTravel](https://blog.wego.com/philippines-etravel-registration/); [SBS Filipino explainer](https://www.sbs.com.au/language/filipino/en/podcast-episode/pano-ba-how-to-register-for-etravel-and-extend-your-stay-in-the-philippines-if-you-are-a-foreign-passport-holder/4bzuud16f)
- Third-party sites charge for eTravel. The only legitimate site is etravel.gov.ph. — [bucket-list-ideas](https://bucket-list-ideas.pages.dev/blog/philippines-etravel-2026-arrival-card-americans/)
- Visa-free entry for up to 30 days applies to the US, UK, Canada, Australia, EU and others. Counts of eligible countries vary from 157 to 162. India and China have special rules. — [Visa Traveler](https://www.visatraveler.com/visa/philippines-visa-guide/); [Mad Monkey visa guide](https://madmonkeyhostels.com/blogs/philippines-visa-guide-tourist-entry-requirements/)
- Passport must be valid 6 months beyond the stay. A return or onward ticket is needed, and a domestic flight does not count as onward. — [Mad Monkey](https://madmonkeyhostels.com/blogs/philippines-visa-guide-tourist-entry-requirements/)
- A 29-day extension (59 days total) is available at Bureau of Immigration (BI) offices or via e-services.immigration.gov.ph. Quoted fees conflict: ₱3,060, ₱3,150 or ₱3,840. — [Visa Traveler](https://www.visatraveler.com/visa/philippines/); [Teleport](https://useteleport.com/blog/philippines-tourist-visa-extension)

### Cited Findings (per destination)
- **El Nido:** Eco-Tourism Development Fee (ETDF) is ₱400 per person, valid 10 days. It rose from ₱200 on 1 June 2024 (Municipal Ordinance 01 s.2024). Students pay ₱300, seniors and PWD ₱320, ages 6–13 ₱250, under 6 free, and Palawan residents ₱200. Pay it before the first boat and keep the receipt. Not included in tour prices: Big Lagoon ₱200, Small Lagoon ₱200, kayak ₱300–400, Matinloc ₱200. Rechecked 1 Nov 2026. — [Suroy.ph El Nido](https://suroy.ph/el-nido-travel-guide/); [The Poor Traveler](https://www.thepoortraveler.net/el-nido-things-to-do/)
- **El Nido 2026 rules:** motorized boats are barred inside the lagoons, daily caps apply at Big Lagoon, Small Lagoon and Secret Beach (agencies pre-book slots), there is a no-swimming order for the town-front water, and drones are restricted within 10 km of Lio Airport. — [Suroy.ph](https://suroy.ph/el-nido-travel-guide/)
- **Coron:** an environmental fee of about ₱200 is commonly reported, paid in cash at Busuanga airport (one source says about ₱198, also charged to ferry arrivals). Sources are thin and conflicting. A 2026 cost guide lists only a ₱100 Calauit Safari fee. — [El Nido/Coron search synthesis incl. Palawan Daily News](https://palawandailynews.com/provincial-news/coron-install-modular-floating-docks/); [Suroy Palawan cost](https://suroy.ph/palawan-trip-cost/)
- **Boracay:** Caticlan Jetty Port terminal fee is ₱150 each way. The environmental fee is ₱150 for Filipinos and ₱300 for foreigners (some sources say ₱300 flat). Aklanons and young children are exempt. Collection is moving to Boracay iPass (boracayipass.ph). A hotel booking confirmation is reportedly checked, and only DOT-accredited hotels should be booked. Under DENR AO 2025-36 (11 Feb 2026), environmental rules for establishments were tightened. — [Tripwis 2026](https://tripwis.com/things-to-know-before-visiting-boracay-philippines); [Respicio commentary](https://www.respicio.ph/commentaries/updated-boracay-travel-requirements-and-entry-regulations-for-tourists); [Context.ph DENR](https://context.ph/2026/02/11/denr-tightens-environmental-rules-for-boracay-impacting-tourism-projects/)
- **Boracay fees under review:** DOT-6 announced a review of all Boracay fees. — [Daily Guardian](https://www.dailyguardian.com.ph/blog/excessive-charges-all-fees-in-boracay-under-review-dot-6-says2sdzxc)
- **Bohol, Chocolate Hills (Carmen):** ₱100–150 per person, sources disagree. A planned increase scheduled for 1 Oct 2025 was deferred by the Carmen mayor, and the current rate is unconfirmed. — [Jonny Melon 2026](https://www.jonnymelon.com/chocolate-hills-bohol/); [Bohol Island News](https://boholislandnews.com/2025/09/18/fee-hike-at-chocolate-hills-sparks-concern-gov-aumentado-appeals-for-status-quo/)
- **Banaue:** environmental fee is ₱20 (older LGU notice) or ₱50 (Wikivoyage, 2021 post, and a SunStar report on standardizing at ₱50). Batad charges a separate ₱50 environmental and heritage fee on the path in. — [Banaue LGU notice](https://banaue.gov.ph/?p=802); [Wikivoyage Banaue](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Banaue); [SunStar](https://www.sunstar.com.ph/more-articles/banaue-moves-to-amend-tourism-laws)
- **Puerto Princesa Underground River (DIY):** entrance ₱500, boat ₱350, eco fee ₱150, audio ₱85. — [Suroy Palawan cost](https://suroy.ph/palawan-trip-cost/)

### Inferences
- Fees are set by each municipality or province and change by ordinance. Guides should list them in a small table with "verified [month year]" and link to the LGU or tourism office.
- Cash in pesos is the safe assumption for every environmental fee.

### Gaps
- eTravel's exact timing window and the current BI extension fee were not confirmed from official pages.
- No 2026 figures were found for Siargao, Panglao, Batanes or Moalboal/Oslob environmental fees.

## 3. Getting there and around

### Takeaway
Big 2025–26 change: Manila (NAIA) no longer has direct flights to El Nido (ended 29 Mar 2026) or, per one source, to Siargao. Both are now reached via Clark, Cebu and others. Typical patterns are a flight plus a van (Puerto Princesa to El Nido, 5–6 hrs) and ferries that weather can cancel. Last-mile transport is tricycles and habal-habal at negotiated fares, and Grab is absent on most islands.

### Cited Findings
- **El Nido:** direct Manila–El Nido flights ended on 29 Mar 2026 and moved to Clark. Lio Airport is served from Clark (about 1h35, 4–5 flights a day, ₱3,500–8,000 one way), Cebu (since 26 Oct 2025), Caticlan, Bohol and Coron. AirSWIFT flights have been operated by Cebgo (code DG) since 1 Jul 2026. Baggage limits on these ATR turboprops are 15 kg checked and 7 kg cabin, weighed strictly. A free Genesis shuttle runs from NAIA T3 to Clark for passengers flying within 24 hrs (since 1 Apr 2026). — [Suroy.ph](https://suroy.ph/el-nido-travel-guide/)
- **Puerto Princesa to El Nido:** shared van ₱720–790, 5–5.5 hrs, departing roughly 3am to mid-afternoon. Cherry Bus ₱651–998, about 6 hrs. Vans arrive at the Corong Corong terminal, so add a tricycle for about ₱100. — [Suroy.ph](https://suroy.ph/el-nido-travel-guide/)
- **Coron to El Nido ferry:** about 3.5 hrs, ₱2,750–3,250. Only about two crossings a week from June to September. The backup is a Busuanga–Lio flight (about 40 min, ~₱3,850). — [Suroy.ph](https://suroy.ph/el-nido-travel-guide/)
- **Coron:** airport van ₱150–250 per seat. Clark–Busuanga return ₱1,500–4,000. — [Suroy Palawan cost](https://suroy.ph/palawan-trip-cost/)
- **Siargao:** Sayak Airport (IAO). One 2026 source says Manila flights ended, with Cebu Pacific moving to Clark in 2025 and PAL dropping Clark in May 2026, leaving service from Clark, Cebu and Davao. Klook still lists Manila flights, so this conflict needs checking. The airport to General Luna takes 45–60 min, shared van ₱300–500, private van ₱2,000–2,700. There is no Grab. — [Suroy how to get to Siargao](https://suroy.ph/how-to-get-to-siargao/); [Klook](https://www.klook.com/en-PH/blog/siargao-for-beginner-surfers/); [Philippines Embassy Info Siargao](https://philippinesembassyinfo.com/tourism/siargao)
- **Siargao habal-habal fares** are quoted inconsistently (₱20–50 short hops, ₱200–500 Dapa to General Luna), so agree the price first. A helmet is legally required on scooters. — [PhilippineBeaches](https://philippinebeaches.org/?p=10409); [Ariana Adventures](https://arianadventures.blogspot.com/2018/02)
- **Banaue:** Ohayami runs overnight from Sampaloc, Manila at about 22:00 and from Banaue at about 18:00–19:00. The trip is 8–11.5 hrs, about US$17–18. Buses are old and the air conditioning is very cold. For Batad, take a jeepney to the Saddle (₱200 tourist fare, May 2024), then trek downhill about 40 min. — [GoAsia Manila–Banaue](https://goasia.cc/en/philippines/manila-to-banaue); [12Go](https://12go.asia/en/bus/manila/banaue/); [Wikivoyage](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Banaue)
- **Batad 2026:** a June 2026 Philstar report calls the Batad terraces "disaster-hit" and urges presidential intervention. Check trail and terrace conditions. — [Philstar](https://www.philstar.com/headlines/2026/06/27/2538081/president-marcos-urged-save-disaster-hit-batad-rice-terraces/amp/)
- **Batanes:** Manila–Basco flights are repeatedly cancelled by monsoons and typhoons (Inquirer reports 2013–2019). — [Inquirer](https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/918315/pal-express-cancels-batanes-flights-due-to-bad-weather/amp)
- **Manila airport taxis:** in Aug 2026 a tourist was allegedly charged ₱5,500 between NAIA terminals, and the LTFRB issued a show-cause order. Use Grab, the coupon taxi desk, or metered taxis only. — [AutoIndustriya](https://www.autoindustriya.com/auto-industry-news/thai-tourists-alleged-php-55k-naia-taxi-fare-prompts-ltfrb-sco.html); [Top Gear PH](https://www.topgear.com.ph/features/feature-articles/guide-avoid-airport-taxi-scams-a4682-20231222-lfrm2)

### Inferences
- For each destination, give the gateway airport(s) with the date of route changes, the transfer time and cost to the main town, the sea option and its seasonal frequency, a backup if weather cancels, and the local transport type with typical fares.
- Recommend buffer days before international departures from weather-exposed islands (Batanes, Palawan ferries, Siargao).

### Gaps
- Current Batanes carriers and schedules for 2026, and current Boracay (Caticlan/Kalibo) transfer prices, were not retrieved.

## 4. Weather

### Takeaway
The national rule of thumb is dry December–May and rainy June–November (PAGASA), with typhoons mostly June–November and peaking July–October. Regions differ a lot. Western Luzon and western Palawan (Type I) have a clear dry season. The east coasts, including Siargao (Type II), get their heaviest rain December–February. Boracay (Type III) has a short dry season.

### Cited Findings
- PAGASA: rainy season June–November, dry season December–May. — [PAGASA climate page](https://bagong.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/information/climate-philippines)
- Modified Coronas climate types: Type I (Metro Manila, Ilocos, western Palawan) is dry Nov–Apr and wettest Jun–Sep. Type II (Eastern Samar, Bicol, Surigao coast incl. Siargao) has no dry season and peak rain Dec–Feb. Type III (Aklan/Boracay, Siquijor) has a short 1–3 month dry season. Type IV has rain spread evenly through the year. — [Wikipedia: Climate of the Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate_of_the_Philippines); [Suroy best time](https://suroy.ph/best-time-to-visit-philippines/)
- About 20 typhoons a year, mostly June–November. Monitor PAGASA and NDRRMC. Updated 15 Jul 2026. — [UK FCDO](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/philippines)
- El Nido: February–March is best. July–October is hardest (August peak), and tours are cancelled about 1 day in 5 to 1 in 3 in wet months. Tour C is cancelled first. — [Suroy.ph](https://suroy.ph/el-nido-travel-guide/)
- Siargao: one guide says March–October to avoid typhoons, while surf season is August–November. — [Globe blog](https://www.globe.com.ph/blog/things-to-do-in-siargao); [Philippines Embassy Info](https://philippinesembassyinfo.com/tourism/siargao)
- 2026–27 outlook (secondary source): PAGASA declared the northeast monsoon on 27 Oct 2026 and expects below-normal rain through January with El Niño. Verify with PAGASA. — [Suroy best time](https://suroy.ph/best-time-to-visit-philippines/)

### Inferences
- Each guide needs its own "best months" line based on its climate type. A single national "dry season" claim is wrong for Siargao and the eastern Visayas.

### Gaps
- No PAGASA month-by-month tables were retrieved for Batanes, Bohol or Cebu.

## 5. Money

### Takeaway
Cash is king outside Manila and Cebu. Island ATMs are few, often reject foreign cards, and run dry on weekends and during fiestas. Card payments carry surcharges of about 3–6% where accepted. Bring pesos for fees, tours and transport.

### Cited Findings
- Siargao: few ATMs (Dapa, General Luna), and the General Luna ATM often accepts only Philippine cards. ATMs run dry on weekends. Bring ₱5,000–10,000 for 5 days. Some resorts charge a card surcharge of at least 6%. — [PhilippineBeaches](https://philippinebeaches.org/?p=10409); [Lakwatsero](https://www.lakwatsero.com/destinations/travel-guide-siargao-island/comment-page-1/)
- El Nido and Coron: ATMs run out ("reliably on weekends and during fiesta" in Coron), and cards are patchy outside hotels. Budget ₱12,000–15,000 in cash for 4 mid-range days in El Nido. — [Suroy Palawan cost](https://suroy.ph/palawan-trip-cost/)
- Palawan 4D/3N mid-range per-person budgets (excluding airfare, Aug 2026): El Nido ₱14,330–24,550, Coron ₱11,920–22,030, Puerto Princesa ₱10,880–20,780. Meals ₱400–2,200 a day. — [Suroy Palawan cost](https://suroy.ph/palawan-trip-cost/)
- Use bank-attached ATMs to reduce skimming risk. — [PhilippineBeaches](https://philippinebeaches.org/?p=10409)
- Street money changers have been reported palming notes. Use established outlets and count before leaving. — [World Nomads](https://www.worldnomads.com/travel-safety/southeast-asia/philippines/what-are-the-common-scams-in-philippines); [TheSmartLocal](https://thesmartlocal.ph/scams-in-the-philippines/amp)

### Inferences
- Each guide could include a "cash needed" line with the per-day estimate and the nearest reliable ATM. Country-wide, advise withdrawing in Manila, Cebu or Clark before flying to small islands.

### Gaps
- No verified data on GCash/Maya acceptance for foreigners, or on country-wide budget tiers.

## 6. Connectivity and power

### Takeaway
Tourist SIMs and eSIMs must be registered with a passport under the SIM Registration Act (RA 11934), and tourist registrations last 30 days. Globe and Smart have counters at NAIA, Mactan-Cebu and Clark. Signal is fine in towns and absent at sea. Palawan's isolated grid has long brownouts.

### Cited Findings
- Tourist SIM registration needs a passport, an address (hotel booking) and a return ticket. It is valid 30 days and extendable with a visa extension. This also applies to eSIMs. — [ExpatDen SIM registration](https://www.expatden.com/philippines/sim-registration-philippines-foreigners/); [Celle eSIM, Globe 30-day rule](https://cellesim.com/en/blog/globe-esim-philippines-tourist-guide-sim-registration-30-day-rule)
- Globe and Smart counters are at NAIA T1–3, Mactan-Cebu and Clark, and a purchase takes about 10–20 min. Smart is often recommended for Palawan and Siargao (a commercial claim, unverified). — [Saily airport SIM guide](https://saily.com/blog/philippines-airport-sim-card/)
- El Nido: signal is decent in town and absent on the water. A grid failure on 22 May 2026 cut power along 240 km of Palawan for about 13 hrs, and El Nido has a history of brownouts, so check that accommodation has a generator. — [Suroy.ph](https://suroy.ph/el-nido-travel-guide/)

### Inferences
- Each guide should note signal (town vs. boat tours) and generator/backup power when recommending stays. Country-wide: get a dual-network setup (Globe plus Smart) for islands.

### Gaps
- No independent 2026 carrier coverage data. Starlink or wifi reliability at specific islands was not found.

## 7. Safety, health, etiquette, scams

### Takeaway
The main tourist destinations are not under elevated advisories. The UK advises against all travel to western/central Mindanao and Sulu, and against all but essential travel to the rest of Mindanao, except Siargao, Camiguin and Dinagat. Practical risks are typhoons, boat safety, airport taxi and money-changer scams, short-changing, and ATM skimming.

### Cited Findings
- UK FCDO (updated 15 Jul 2026, still current 16 Nov 2026): advises against all travel to western and central Mindanao and the Sulu archipelago, and against all but essential travel to the rest of Mindanao except Camiguin, Dinagat and Siargao. Large demonstrations are expected in Metro Manila. Travel against FCDO advice can invalidate insurance. — [UK FCDO](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/philippines)
- Common scams: taxis that refuse the meter or quote fixed prices, drivers who claim "no change", jeepney short-changing, and sleight-of-hand money changers. — [World Nomads](https://www.worldnomads.com/travel-safety/southeast-asia/philippines/what-are-the-common-scams-in-philippines); [Top Gear PH](https://www.topgear.com.ph/features/feature-articles/guide-avoid-airport-taxi-scams-a4682-20231222-lfrm2)
- Fake eTravel sites charge up to about US$100. — [bucket-list-ideas](https://bucket-list-ideas.pages.dev/blog/philippines-etravel-2026-arrival-card-americans/)
- Helmets are legally required on scooters (Siargao). — [Siargao search synthesis, PhilippineBeaches](https://philippinebeaches.org/?p=10409)
- El Nido has a 2026 no-swimming order for the town-front water. — [Suroy.ph](https://suroy.ph/el-nido-travel-guide/)

### Inferences
- Country-wide content should cover advisories, the Mindanao nuance (Siargao is exempt), typhoon protocol, scams, eTravel fakes, and insurance. Per-destination content should cover local hazards (water quality, scooter safety, boat cancellations).

### Gaps
- The US State Dept page returned 403 and Smartraveller timed out, so their advisory levels and health guidance (dengue, rabies, water) are not cited here.
- No sourced findings on etiquette (dress at churches, "po/opo", haggling norms) or local laws (drugs, vaping and smoking bans). These need a follow-up pass.
