f:Td84,<p>Hidden beneath limestone mountains and dense rainforest, the Puerto Princesa Underground River is one of Palawan’s most extraordinary experiences. This 8.2-kilometer subterranean river flows directly into the sea, winding through cathedral-like caverns filled with rock formations, stalactites, and bats. Recognized as one of the New 7 Wonders of Nature, it’s a must-visit for travelers exploring central Palawan.</p>
<h2>Quick Facts:</h2>
<ul>
<li>Region: Palawan (Central)</li>
<li>Best Time to Visit: November to May (dry season)</li>
<li>How to Get There: Fly to Puerto Princesa City, then take a 2-hour van or bus ride to Sabang</li>
<li>Ideal Stay: 2–3 days</li>
</ul>
<h2>Summary</h2>
<ul>
<li>UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the New 7 Wonders of Nature</li>
<li>8.2 km-long underground river system accessible by boat</li>
<li>Ideal for eco-tourism and day trips from Puerto Princesa City</li>
<li>Best visited during dry months for calm river conditions</li>
</ul>
<h2>Top Attractions:</h2>
<ol>
<li>Underground River Tour — Explore the limestone caverns by paddleboat, guided by local boatmen trained by the park.</li>
<li>Sabang Mangrove Paddle Tour — A relaxing ride through a mangrove forest, home to birds, snakes, and monkeys.</li>
<li>Ugong Rock Adventures — Try ziplining or spelunking at this limestone formation en route to Sabang.</li>
<li>Sabang Beach — A scenic, less-crowded beach perfect for post-tour relaxation.</li>
<li>Palawan Wildlife Rescue and Conservation Center — See crocodiles and learn about local wildlife conservation.</li>
</ol>
<h2>How to Get There:</h2>
<ul>
<li>By Air: Fly to Puerto Princesa International Airport (PPS).</li>
<li>By Land: From Puerto Princesa City, take a van or bus to Sabang (approx. 2 hours).</li>
<li>By Tour: Most hotels and agencies offer organized day tours with transport, permits, and lunch included.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Best Time to Visit:</h2>
<ul>
<li>Visit from November to May, during the dry season. The river is calmer, and tours are rarely canceled due to weather.</li>
<li>Avoid July to October when heavy rainfall can cause flooding inside the cave.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Where to Stay:</h2>
<ul>
<li>Daluyon Beach and Mountain Resort — eco-luxury resort close to the park entrance</li>
<li>Sheridan Beach Resort &#x26; Spa — beachfront resort with full amenities</li>
<li>Bambua Nature Cottages — eco-friendly lodging surrounded by greenery</li>
</ul>
<h2>Where to eat:</h2>
<ul>
<li>Gusto Grill &#x26; Resto — grilled seafood and Filipino favorites</li>
<li>The Smoky Place — wood-fired pizza near Sabang Beach</li>
<li>Daluyon Café — healthy local and international dishes with ocean views</li>
</ul>
<h2>Tips &#x26; Local Hacks:</h2>
<ul>
<li>Secure your Underground River permit at least a day before — required by the park office.</li>
<li>Bring a dry bag for valuables during the boat ride.</li>
<li>Wear sandals or waterproof shoes — some parts of the tour may get wet.</li>
<li>Combine the river tour with Ugong Rock or the mangrove tour to maximize your trip.</li>
<li>Keep voices low inside the cave — sound echoes easily!</li>
</ul>
<h2>3-Day Suggested Itinerary:</h2>
<ul>
<li>Day 1: Arrival in Puerto Princesa → Visit Crocodile Farm → Dinner at KaLui Restaurant</li>
<li>Day 2: Underground River Tour + Sabang Beach afternoon swim</li>
<li>Day 3: Ugong Rock zipline + return to city → Optional Honda Bay island hopping</li>
