f:Tece,<p>Coron is where adventure meets serenity, a stunning mix of limestone cliffs, turquoise lagoons, and crystal lakes that feel untouched by time. Located on Busuanga Island in northern Palawan, Coron is a paradise for divers, nature lovers, and anyone looking for raw island beauty. From snorkeling WWII shipwrecks to exploring hidden lakes surrounded by cliffs, Coron offers an unforgettable slice of the Philippines.</p>
<h2>Quick Facts:</h2>
<ul>
<li>Region: Palawan (Busuanga area)</li>
<li>Best Time to Visit: December to May (dry season)</li>
<li>How to Get There: Fly directly to Busuanga (Coron) Airport from Manila or Cebu</li>
<li>Ideal Stay: 3–5 days</li>
</ul>
<h2>Summary</h2>
<ul>
<li>Known for world-famous Kayangan Lake and Twin Lagoon</li>
<li>Top diving destination with WWII shipwrecks and coral gardens</li>
<li>Accessible via Busuanga Airport</li>
<li>Perfect for eco-tourism, photography, and adventure seekers</li>
</ul>
<h2>Top Attractions:</h2>
<ol>
<li>Kayangan Lake — Known as the cleanest lake in Asia, surrounded by limestone walls and accessible via a short hike.</li>
<li>Twin Lagoon — Two lagoons separated by limestone cliffs; swim through a small opening or climb over for a surreal experience.</li>
<li>Barracuda Lake — A unique diving spot where freshwater meets saltwater, creating shifting temperatures.</li>
<li>Siete Pecados Marine Park — Ideal for snorkeling with vibrant coral gardens and schools of fish.</li>
<li>Coron Bay Shipwrecks — Dive among historic WWII wrecks now covered in coral and marine life.</li>
<li>Mt. Tapyas Viewpoint — Climb 700 steps for a panoramic view of Coron town and the islands beyond.</li>
<li>Maquinit Hot Springs — Natural saltwater hot springs perfect for relaxation after a day of island hopping.</li>
</ol>
<h2>How to Get There:</h2>
<ul>
<li>By Air: Fly to Busuanga Airport (Francisco B. Reyes Airport) from Manila (approx. 1 hour) or Cebu.</li>
<li>By Sea: Ferries operate from Manila or El Nido to Coron (6–8 hours).</li>
<li>Getting Around: Tricycles are the main transport in town, and island-hopping tours can be booked easily from local operators.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Best Time to Visit:</h2>
<ul>
<li>Dry months from December to May offer calm waters, clear skies, and ideal visibility for diving and snorkeling.</li>
<li>The rainy season (June–October) is still manageable but expect some afternoon showers and occasional tour cancellations.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Where to Stay:</h2>
<ul>
<li>Two Seasons Coron Island Resort &#x26; Spa — luxury stay with private beachfront</li>
<li>The Funny Lion — boutique hotel with a rooftop jacuzzi and bay views</li>
<li>Hop Hostel — budget-friendly with a lively rooftop bar</li>
</ul>
<h2>Where to eat:</h2>
<ul>
<li>KT’s Sinugba Sa Balay — local Filipino favorites</li>
<li>Altrove Coron — pizza and pasta from a wood-fired oven</li>
<li>Brujita — vegetarian and vegan-friendly dishes</li>
<li>Levine’s Café — perfect breakfast spot with a view</li>
</ul>
<h2>Tips &#x26; Local Hacks:</h2>
<ul>
<li>Bring cash, as some tours and restaurants don’t accept cards.</li>
<li>Book island-hopping packages (Coron Ultimate Tour or Super Ultimate Tour) — they include the best highlights.</li>
<li>Drone use may require a local permit in certain areas.</li>
<li>Visit Kayangan Lake early morning to avoid crowds.</li>
<li>Rent snorkeling gear instead of buying — easy to find in town.</li>
</ul>
<h2>3-Day Suggested Itinerary:</h2>
<ul>
<li>Day 1: Arrive → Mt. Tapyas hike → Sunset at the viewpoint → Dinner in town</li>
<li>Day 2: Coron Ultimate Tour (Kayangan Lake, Twin Lagoon, Siete Pecados) → Maquinit Hot Springs</li>
<li>Day 3: Wreck Diving or Island Hopping (Malcapuya, Banana, Bulog Dos) → Souvenir shopping</li>
