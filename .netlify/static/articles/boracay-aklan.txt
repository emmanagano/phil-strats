f:Teb8,<p>Boracay is more than just a beach — it’s a tropical escape. With its powdery white sand, crystal-clear waters, and epic sunsets, this Aklan island ranks among the Philippines’ top vacation destinations. After its 2018 rehabilitation, Boracay has reinvented itself as a model of sustainable tourism and laid-back luxury. Whether you’re chasing sunrises, island hopping, or barefoot dinners by the shore, Boracay delivers unforgettable moments.</p>
<h2>Quick Facts:</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Region</strong>: Western Visayas</li>
<li><strong>Best Time to Visit</strong>: November to May</li>
<li><strong>How to Get There</strong>: Fly to Caticlan (Boracay Airport) or Kalibo, then take a short ferry to the island</li>
<li><strong>Ideal Stay</strong>: 3–5 days</li>
</ul>
<h2>Summary</h2>
<ul>
<li>Famous for its 4-km stretch of White Beach and epic sunsets</li>
<li>Rebuilt with eco-friendly tourism standards after 2018 rehabilitation</li>
<li>Great for swimming, island hopping, and beachfront dining</li>
<li>Easy access via Caticlan Airport</li>
<li>Wide range of stays — from budget hostels to luxury resorts</li>
</ul>
<h2>Top Attractions:</h2>
<ol>
<li>White Beach (Stations 1–3): The heart of Boracay — ideal for swimming, walks, and sunset views.</li>
<li>Puka Shell Beach: A quieter, natural stretch known for its coarse sand and laid-back vibe.</li>
<li>Ariel’s Point: A must-try for cliff diving and adrenaline seekers.</li>
<li>Mount Luho Viewpoint: Offers a panoramic view of the island.</li>
<li>Bulabog Beach: The kite-surfing and windsurfing hub, best during amihan season.</li>
<li>Island Hopping: Crocodile Island, Crystal Cove, and Magic Island for snorkeling and picnics.</li>
</ol>
<h2>How to Get There:</h2>
<p>Fly to Caticlan Airport (MPH) for the quickest access — only a 10-minute ride to Caticlan Jetty Port, then a 15-minute boat trip to Boracay.</p>
<p>Alternatively, flights to Kalibo International Airport (KLO) are cheaper but require a 1.5-hour land transfer to the port.</p>
<p>Once on the island, tricycles and e-trikes are the main transport — though most areas are walkable along White Beach.</p>
<h2>Best Time to Visit:</h2>
<p>November to May is the dry season with calm seas and cool breezes — ideal for swimming and sunsets.
Avoid July to October if possible, as typhoons can disrupt travel.</p>
<p>For fewer crowds but great weather, travel between February and April.</p>
<h2>Where to Stay:</h2>
<ul>
<li>Station 1: Premium resorts and quieter ambiance (The Lind, Discovery Shores)</li>
<li>Station 2: Central area near D’Mall, nightlife, and midrange hotels</li>
<li>Station 3: Budget stays and a more relaxed atmosphere</li>
</ul>
<h2>Where to eat:</h2>
<ul>
<li>Sunny Side Café — famous for breakfast and beachfront pancakes</li>
<li>Dos Mestizos — Spanish-Filipino fusion</li>
<li>Los Indios Bravos — craft beer and hearty meals</li>
<li>Cha Cha’s Beach Café — tropical cocktails with sunset views</li>
</ul>
<h2>Tips &#x26; Local Hacks:</h2>
<ul>
<li>Bring reef-safe sunscreen (it’s a requirement on the island).</li>
<li>Avoid bringing single-use plastics — Boracay enforces strict eco rules.</li>
<li>Book sunset sailing (paraw) around 5 PM for best photos.</li>
<li>Use e-trikes instead of regular tricycles — quieter and eco-friendly.</li>
<li>Walk from Station 1 to Station 3 for the full White Beach experience.</li>
</ul>
<h2>3-Day Suggested Itinerary:</h2>
<ul>
<li>Day 1: Arrive → Check in → Walk White Beach → Sunset paraw → Dinner by the shore</li>
<li>Day 2: Island hopping (Crocodile Island, Crystal Cove) → Puka Beach → D’Mall at night</li>
<li>Day 3: Mount Luho viewpoint → Chill morning swim → Brunch → Souvenir shopping → Depart</li>
