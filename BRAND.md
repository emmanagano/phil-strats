# Phil Strats brand voice

How Phil Strats sounds. Use it for every guide, news brief, showbiz story, page and email. Visual rules (palette, type, layout) live in `CLAUDE.md`.

## Personality

A well-traveled friend who has just come back from the island you're planning: warm and specific, tells you the real price and the catch, and respects the places and people they write about.

## Voice attributes

**Honest**
- We are: upfront about costs, crowds, weather and safety. Uncertain numbers get `≈`; every guide shows when it was last checked.
- We are not: hype. No "paradise", "hidden gem" or "best in the country" unless a source says so.
- Sounds like: "It's not a white-sand paradise, though: expect rocky islands and busy weekends."
- Not like: "The most breathtaking islands you'll ever see!"

**Practical**
- We are: built for planning. Every page answers when to go, how to get there, what it costs and what to watch out for.
- We are not: a mood board. Description earns its place by helping a decision.
- Sounds like: "Be at Ivana Port by 6–6:30am. Boats leave early and don't follow a fixed timetable."
- Not like: "Let the gentle waves carry you to a world of wonder."

**Warm**
- We are: second person, conversational, encouraging. Contractions are fine.
- We are not: cutesy or slangy. No "lol", no stacked exclamation marks.
- Sounds like: "Go on a calm morning and you'll have the arch almost to yourself."
- Not like: "OMG you NEED to see this!!!"

**Respectful**
- We are: careful with culture, religion, indigenous communities and burial sites. We name communities correctly (Ivatan, Ifugao, Kankanaey, T'boli) and ask readers to ask before photographing people.
- We are not: exoticizing. People are hosts, not scenery.
- Sounds like: "Treat it as a burial site: keep your distance and don't touch anything."

## Messaging pillars

1. **Plan it in one place.** Interactive guides: months, routes, budgets, itineraries.
2. **What it really costs.** Peso budgets for three travel styles, honest fees.
3. **One island at a time.** Depth over listicles, beyond the famous five.
4. **Travel with care.** Safety advisories, weather buffers, respect for communities.

## Claims and sourcing

- No unsupported superlatives ("best", "longest", "richest", "only"). Use them only when a credible source says so, and attribute: "ranked by marine scientists among…", "the Daily Tribune called it the largest batch yet".
- Prefer the checkable fact over the adjective: "dual 840-meter ziplines", not "Asia's longest ziplines".
- Prices, fees and fares that may change get `≈` and live under a dated `guide.verified`.
- Mindanao destinations always carry current government travel advice, dated and attributed (for example "As of Nov 2026, the UK FCDO advises…").
- News and showbiz: cite sources, write still-changing counts as early figures ("at least 18"), never predict results or audience reactions, no rumors or private lives.
- No promises we don't keep: no publishing cadence ("every week") and no review or rating markup without real reviews.

## Style rules

| Rule | Phil Strats style |
|---|---|
| Oxford comma | Yes: "beaches, caves, and coffee" |
| Headings | Sentence case: "Best time to visit Batad" |
| Dashes in prose | No em dashes. Use a colon, comma, semicolon or a new sentence. (Title separators like "Phil Strats — Tagline" are fine.) |
| Ranges | En dash, no spaces: "₱500–1,000", "Mar–May", "2–3 hrs" |
| Money | Peso sign, no space, commas: "₱1,500". USD only in the budget calculator |
| Approximate | "≈ ₱100" (sign, space, amount) |
| Times | "6am", "4:30pm"; durations "45 min", "2 hrs" |
| Months | Short in data ("Mar–May"), full in sentences ("March to May") |
| Numbers | Numerals for prices, times, distances and counts over nine |
| Exclamation marks | Rare; never two in a row |
| Links | Descriptive text: "our Batad trek guide", never "click here" |

## Terminology

| Use | Not | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| faluwa | falowa | The Batanes boat; same spelling everywhere |
| habal-habal | habal habal | Motorbike taxi |
| Holy Week | Semana Santa | Mention it as a crowd warning |
| Cagayan de Oro | CDO | Spell it out in copy |
| Ivatan, Ifugao, Kankanaey, T'boli | "tribe", "natives" | Name the community |
| etravel.gov.ph | eTravel app links | Always warn against paid look-alike sites |
| guide (our pages) | blog post, article | In copy; `articles` stays in URLs |

## Tone by place

| Where | Tone |
|---|---|
| Guide verdict | Two or three sentences: who it's for, who should skip it |
| Things to do, tips | Short, imperative, specific |
| Story behind the guide | The most descriptive part, still grounded in facts |
| Safety and advisories | Plain, calm, dated, attributed |
| News briefs | Neutral and factual; "For your trip" notes stay practical |
| Showbiz | Upbeat but verified: achievements, releases, events |
| Newsletter and CTAs | Friendly, low-pressure: "No spam. Unsubscribe anytime." |
