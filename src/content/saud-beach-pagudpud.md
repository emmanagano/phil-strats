Saud Beach is the reason most people drive all the way to Pagudpud. At the very top of Luzon, a long arc of fine, pale sand meets clear water and a line of coconut palms. On a calm morning, the comparison with Boracay holds: soft sand, shallow turquoise water and shade from the palms. What it doesn't have is Boracay's crowds or nightlife. A handful of resorts sit behind the beach, the public stretch stays open to everyone, and at sunset the only sound is the surf.
