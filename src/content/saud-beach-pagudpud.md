Saud Beach is the reason most people drive all the way to Pagudpud. At the very top of Luzon, a long arc of fine, pale sand meets clear water and a line of coconut palms, with far fewer people than the Philippines' famous island beaches. This guide covers when to go, what it costs, and how Saud compares with Pagudpud's other beaches. For the full trip, including the Bangui windmills, Kapurpurawan and the drive from Laoag, see our [Pagudpud travel guide](/articles/pagudpud-ilocos-norte).

## Why Saud Beach is special

Saud is often called the Boracay of the north, and on a calm morning the comparison holds: soft sand, shallow turquoise water and shade from the palms. What it doesn't have is Boracay's crowds or nightlife. A handful of resorts sit behind the beach, the public stretch stays open to everyone, and at sunset the only sound is the surf.

## Best time to visit Saud Beach

| Months | Conditions |
|---|---|
| November–February | Cool and breezy. Usually calm, with some windy days from the northeast monsoon. |
| March–May | Hot, sunny and calm: the best swimming. Holy Week is busy. |
| June–October | Rainy season, with stronger waves and typhoon risk. Quiet and cheap, but swim carefully. |

Weekdays are noticeably quieter than weekends all year.

## Fees and costs

- **Environmental fee:** ≈ ₱20–50 per person, collected by the municipality
- **Cottage or table rental:** ≈ ₱350–1,000 per day, depending on the resort
- **Tricycle from the Pagudpud town center:** ≈ ₱100–200 one way
- **Resort rooms:** from about ₱1,500 a night for simple rooms to ₱6,000+ for beachfront resorts

Bring cash in small bills. Tricycles, fees and small eateries rarely take cards or e-wallets.

## Saud Beach vs Pagudpud's other beaches

| Beach | Best for | Vibe |
|---|---|---|
| **Saud Beach** | Swimming, lazy beach days | Calm, palm-shaded, the classic postcard |
| **Blue Lagoon (Maira-ira)** | Surfing, resorts, bright blue water | Livelier, more development, surf from about October to March |
| **Pansian Beach** | Solitude | Remote and undeveloped, farther east toward Cagayan |
| **Hannah's / Belle Beach area** | Resort stays | Rocky coves and a big resort with activities |

If Saud's waves are rough, Blue Lagoon is often calmer on the same day, and the reverse also happens. Ask at your resort in the morning.

## How to get to Saud Beach

1. **Fly or bus to Laoag.** Flights from Manila take about 1 hr 10 min; overnight buses take 9–10 hours.
2. **Bus or van north to Pagudpud.** About 1.5–2 hours along the coast road from Laoag.
3. **Tricycle to Saud.** A short ride from the town center or the highway junction.

Driving yourself? Combine it with Kapurpurawan, the Bangui windmills and the Patapat Viaduct. Our [Vigan and Laoag guide](/articles/vigan-and-laoag-ilocos) covers the southern half of the road trip.

## Where to stay near Saud Beach

- **On Saud Beach:** wake up to the sand. Beachfront resorts range from simple rooms to comfortable villas.
- **Blue Lagoon:** more choice and more surf camps, 20–30 minutes away.
- **Pagudpud town:** the cheapest inns, but you'll need tricycles to reach the beaches.

## Tips for a good beach day

- Swim near other people and respect red flags. There are no lifeguards on most stretches.
- Bring reef-safe sunscreen, a hat and water. Shade disappears at midday.
- Try Ilocano food at the beach eateries: bagnet, pinakbet and grilled fish.
- Take your trash with you. Pagudpud's beaches stay beautiful because visitors do.
