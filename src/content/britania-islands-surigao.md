The Britania Islands are a group of 24 small islets off San Agustin in Surigao del Sur, on Mindanao's Pacific coast. Think jungle-topped islands, white sandbars and clear green water, with hardly another tourist in sight. Pair it with the Enchanted River and Tinuy-an Falls for a full road trip.
