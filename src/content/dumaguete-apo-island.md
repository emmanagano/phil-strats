Dumaguete, the "City of Gentle People," is a relaxed university town on the southeast coast of Negros. It's the gateway to Apo Island's turtle sanctuary, the rainforest crater lakes above Sibulan and the waterfalls around Valencia, with good food and an easy seaside promenade to come home to.
