Port Barton is a small fishing village on Palawan's west coast, halfway between Puerto Princesa and El Nido. It has no big resorts or traffic, just a sandy main street, cheap island tours and calm water full of turtles. It's the perfect place to slow down for a few days.

Planning more than one stop? See our guides to [El Nido](/articles/el-nido-palawan) or [Puerto Princesa](/articles/puerto-princesa-palawan).
