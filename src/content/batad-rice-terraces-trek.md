If you visit the Banaue Rice Terraces, make time for Batad. An hour from Banaue town, this village sits at the bottom of a natural amphitheater of stone-walled terraces, one of the five clusters that make up the UNESCO-listed Rice Terraces of the Philippine Cordilleras. There's no road into the village, so you arrive on foot, which is half the magic. The terraces are still farmed by Ifugao families, with water running through bamboo channels from paddy to paddy as it has for generations.
