Bantayan Island sits off the northern tip of Cebu, a flat, laid-back island with powdery white beaches, calm shallow water and a slower pace than the busier resort islands. Days here are simple: swim, eat grilled seafood, scooter between sleepy towns and catch the sunset from the sand.
