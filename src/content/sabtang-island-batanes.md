Sabtang is where Batanes' Ivatan heritage feels most alive. A short boat ride from Batan Island, this small island has stone villages with thick limestone walls and cogon roofs, built to ride out the strongest typhoons in the country, plus white-sand coves and a famous natural arch at Morong Beach. In Chavayan, at the end of the road, you'll still see farmers in the vakul, the woven palm-fiber head cover of the Ivatan.
