Sagada's limestone hills hide some of the best-known caves in the Philippines. Sumaguing, the "Big Cave", is a barefoot scramble past rimstone pools and giant formations; Lumiang is a burial cave whose mouth is stacked with old coffins; and the cave connection joins the two underground. For the Kankanaey people of Sagada, placing the dead in caves and on cliffs is a living tradition, which is why every visit here is guided and unhurried.
