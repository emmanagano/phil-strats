Perched at about 1,500 meters in the Cordillera, Baguio was built as a cool-weather retreat and still works like one. Pine trees frame its hills, art villages and museums celebrate Igorot culture, and its markets sell strawberries, ube jam and Benguet coffee. Come for the cool air, then use it as the jumping-off point for the mountain provinces.

Heading deeper into the mountains? See our guides to [Sagada](/articles/sagada-mountain-province), [Mount Pulag](/articles/mount-pulag-benguet) and the [Banaue Rice Terraces](/articles/banaue-rice-terraces-ifugao).
