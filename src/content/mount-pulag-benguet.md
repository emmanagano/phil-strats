Mount Pulag is the highest peak on Luzon, a grassy summit that rises above a layer of clouds at dawn. It is a short, rewarding climb for hikers who do not mind a cold, early start.

Planning more than one stop? See our guides to [Sagada](/articles/sagada-mountain-province) or [the Banaue Rice Terraces](/articles/banaue-rice-terraces-ifugao).

## Mount Pulag Quick Facts

- Region: Cordillera (Benguet, Ifugao, and Nueva Vizcaya)
- Best Time to Visit: December to March
- How to Get There: Bus from Manila to Baguio, then a jeepney or van to Babadak Ranger Station
- Ideal Stay: 2 days
- Notable For: Sea of clouds, mossy forest, and grassland summit

## Mount Pulag at a Glance

- Luzon's highest peak with a manageable beginner trail
- Short overnight hike with sunrise at the summit
- Mossy forest, pine, and grassland landscapes
- Managed by local guides and park staff
- Cold weather gear is non-negotiable

## Top Attractions in Mount Pulag

1. Summit Sunrise – The main draw and the reason for the pre-dawn start.
2. Ambangeg Trail – Beginner-friendly route through mossy forest.
3. Akiki Trail – The steeper "killer" route for experienced hikers.
4. Camp 2 – Common campsite before the final ascent.
5. Grassland Plateau – Open views near the top.
6. Baguio City – A cool-climate stop before or after the climb.

## How to Get to Mount Pulag

- Take a bus from Manila to Baguio (about 5–6 hours).
- Take a jeepney or van from Baguio to Babadak Ranger Station in Kabayan.
- Register and attend the briefing, then start the hike with a guide.

## Best Time to Visit Mount Pulag

December to March is generally dry and clear, with the best chance of a sea of clouds. It is also the coldest time of year, so pack accordingly.

## Where to Stay in Mount Pulag

- Homestays in Kabayan – Simple stays near the ranger station.
- Camp 2 – Basic camping for an early summit.
- Hotels in Baguio – More comfort before or after the hike.

## Where to Eat in Mount Pulag

- Kabayan eateries – Hearty local meals.
- Baguio markets – Strawberry taho, vegetables, and snacks.
- Trail snacks – Pack energy bars, nuts, and hot drinks.

## Mount Pulag Travel Tips & Local Hacks

- Bring a headlamp, gloves, and a warm jacket.
- Start training with long walks a few weeks before.
- Respect local guides and follow Leave No Trace rules.
- Check weather and trail advisories before you go.

## 3-Day Mount Pulag Itinerary

- Day 1: Travel to Baguio, then Kabayan, and attend the orientation.
- Day 2: Hike to Camp 2, rest, and begin the summit climb before dawn.
- Day 3: Watch the sunrise, descend, and travel back to Baguio or Manila.
