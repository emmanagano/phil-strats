Bacolod grew rich on sugar, and the boom left Negros Occidental with mansions, ancestral houses and a serious dessert habit. Today the "City of Smiles" is best known for chicken inasal grilled over charcoal, the October MassKara festival, and the haunting shell of The Ruins in neighboring Talisay. It's an easy, friendly city to eat your way through.

Pair it with [Iloilo & Guimaras](/articles/iloilo-guimaras), a short fastcraft ride away, or head south to [Dumaguete & Apo Island](/articles/dumaguete-apo-island).
