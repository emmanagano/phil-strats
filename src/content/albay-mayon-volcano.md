Albay is home to Mayon Volcano, famous for its almost perfect cone, which looms over the Bicol countryside and the town of Legazpi. Base yourself here to see the Cagsawa Ruins, ride ATVs across old lava fields, eat fiery coconut-milk dishes, and take a day trip to swim with Donsol's whale sharks.
