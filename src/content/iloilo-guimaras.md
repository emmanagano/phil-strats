Iloilo City, the "City of Love," pairs grand Spanish-era churches and restored heritage streets with one of the best food scenes in the Philippines. Just across a narrow strait, Guimaras adds mango farms, a hilltop monastery and quiet beaches for an easy day or overnight escape.
