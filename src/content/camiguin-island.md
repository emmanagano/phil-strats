Camiguin packs seven volcanoes, hot and cold springs, waterfalls, and a sandbar into an island you can circle by scooter in a day. It is quiet, friendly, and easy to love.

Planning more than one stop? See our guides to [Siquijor](/articles/siquijor-island) or [Siargao](/articles/siargao-surfing-capital).

## Camiguin Quick Facts

- Region: Northern Mindanao (Camiguin Province)
- Best Time to Visit: March to October for the sandbar, with the Lanzones Festival in October
- How to Get There: Ferry from Balingoan, or fly via Cagayan de Oro
- Ideal Stay: 2–3 days
- Notable For: White Island, hot springs, waterfalls, and volcano views

## Camiguin at a Glance

- Compact island with an easy coastal ring road
- Mix of beaches, hot springs, and hiking
- Warm, low-key local culture
- Affordable and less crowded than better-known islands
- Famous for sweet lanzones fruit

## Top Attractions in Camiguin

1. White Island – Sandbar with panoramic volcano views.
2. Ardent Hot Spring – Warm volcanic pools, lovely at night.
3. Sunken Cemetery – Cross marking a cemetery lost to a volcanic eruption.
4. Katibawasan Falls – Tall waterfall with a cool plunge pool.
5. Tuasan Falls – Turquoise water and a rope swing.
6. Old Vulcan Daan – Giant cross on a hill overlooking the coast.
7. Mantigue Island – Snorkeling and a marine sanctuary.

## How to Get to Camiguin

- Fly to Cagayan de Oro, then travel by bus or van to Balingoan (about 2 hours).
- Take a ferry from Balingoan to Benoni port (about 1–2 hours).
- Rent a scooter or hire a tricycle on the island.

## Best Time to Visit Camiguin

March to October is generally sunny and good for the sandbar. October's Lanzones Festival is lively, but book accommodation early.

## Where to Stay in Camiguin

- Beachfront resorts in Mambajao – Close to White Island boats.
- Guesthouses near the hot springs – Budget-friendly and quiet.
- Eco-resorts on the coast – Garden settings and sea views.

## Where to Eat in Camiguin

- Local seafood grills – Fresh catch near the beach.
- Pastel shops – Soft buns with sweet yema filling, a local favorite.
- Lanzones stalls – Sweet seasonal fruit.
- Small cafés in Mambajao – Coffee and breakfast.

## Camiguin Travel Tips & Local Hacks

- Bring a hat, sunscreen, and snacks for White Island.
- Go to the hot springs in the evening.
- Hire a boatman at Agoho early for calmer water.
- Carry some cash; card payments are limited.

## 3-Day Camiguin Itinerary

- Day 1: Arrive, check in, and soak at Ardent Hot Spring.
- Day 2: Island-hop to White Island and Mantigue, then see the Sunken Cemetery at sunset.
- Day 3: Katibawasan and Tuasan Falls, then head back to the ferry.
